# Resident Associates

Resident Associates—RAs—are the adults who live in the college. The college system brought Wiess two of them in 1957, "one of whom, Dr. John E. Parish, remained as senior resident associate for 23 years" [@riceinfo-history]. His successor in longevity, Dr. William "Dr. Bill" Wilson of Electrical Engineering, was an RA by 1976, interim Magister[^magister] in spring 1983, and still living on the fourth floor in 2005–06—about thirty years, the longest Wiess association in the record, remembered in the name of [Wilson House](../places/wilson-house.md) [@handbook-1994] [@wb 20070709183122 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?module=page&page=resident_associates]. In Old Wiess the RAs were professors; from 2003 the O-Week books also introduce young alumni couples and lecturers living on the third and fourth floors of the central wing [@wb 20040619001755 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/01-14%20intro.pdf p.8]. The Constitution has always seated the RAs on Cabinet without a vote [@constitution-1993]; since 2016 they are chosen through a student-led search committee [@constitution-2016 p.1], and since 2026 they share an article with the Magister, "Residential Associates" [@constitution-2026 p.3].

## Variants & disputes

- **How long Dr. Bill was an RA.** "almost thirty years" in 2003 [@wb 20040619001755 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/01-14%20intro.pdf p.8]; "for countless years" in 2007 [@wb 20070709183122 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?module=page&page=resident_associates]; "almost as long" as his thirty-plus years of teaching, per Kean [@rhc 2014-01-24 friday-afternoon-follies-dr-bill-in-a-skirt]. An alumnus places him among the RAs in the 1976 Campanile [@rhc 2014-11-19 wiess-college-luminaries comment by rcspitzer, 21 Nov 2014], and the 2007 page ends his residence in 2006. Roughly 1975–2006, then; the exact start is open.
- **Dr. Parish's end.** The 1994 history gives 23 years from 1957, which lands in 1980 [@riceinfo-history]; an alumna remembers his death in office "in either '79 or '80" [@rhc 2014-11-19 wiess-college-luminaries comment by Gloria Tarpley '81, 19 Nov 2014]. The two agree. His name is spelled "Parish" by the handbook and "Parrish" by the commenters.
- **"Gone over to the dark side."** The 1994 glossary says John Bennett "Has gone over to the dark side of the Force" without saying where [@handbook-1994]; the War Pig deck's caption calls him "Elec. prof, Wiessman, Wiess associate, Sid Magister" [@warpig-core-deck slide 27]. The deck is testimony compiled c.2024; nothing contemporary in the corpus names his Sid Richardson post.
- **The Parish Grants.** "For self directed travel abroad by Wiessmen and students from other colleges" [@oweek-2010 p.91]. No source in the corpus says they are named for Dr. Parish; the name makes it likely, but it is an inference.
- **Associate or RA.** Kean's 2014 caption calls Stan Dodds "stalwart Wiess associate" [@rhc 2014-11-19 wiess-college-luminaries]; he was a Resident Associate from 1992 to at least 1999 [@handbook-1994] [@riceinfo-associates], and by 2003 was no longer among the three RAs listed [@wb 20040619001755 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/01-14%20intro.pdf p.8].
