# College Idiot & other offices

The College Idiot is Wiess's honorary office: "An honorary office conferred upon one deserving Wiess member each year (or more, if each are deserving in their own unique way). The monetary prize is not as large as in years past, but the awesome responsibility of donning the Great Pumpkin guise on Halloween night remains" [@handbook-1994]. The Idiot is elected each fall, crowned with a pumpkin, and leads [Pumpkin Caroling](../traditions/pumpkin-caroling.md) round the campus [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. It is not an insult: "if you were an asshole, you would've been elected DWE, instead" [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. The office is at least as old as 1979—George Hall held it four years running while also serving as College President in 1981 [@handbook-1994]—and the glossary has carried it in every O-Week book since. The number of Idiots has drifted upward: "one deserving Wiessmen" through 2011, "one deserving upperclassman and one freshman" in 2014, "the elected College Idiots" from 2015 [@oweek-2011 p.92] [@oweek-2014 p.102] [@oweek-2015 p.119]. This page also notes the other named roles the sources show—the Pig Master, the DWE, and a Class Council Historian—none of them Wiess College offices in the constitutional sense.

## As the college described it

!!! quote "1994 Freshman Handbook"
    "**College Idiot.** An honorary office conferred upon one deserving Wiess member each year (or more, if each are deserving in their own unique way). The monetary prize is not as large as in years past, but the awesome responsibility of donning the Great Pumpkin guise on Halloween night remains." [@handbook-1994]

!!! quote "Pumpkin Caroling page, riceinfo site (Ray Wagner, 1999)"
    "A highly-prized honor here at Wiess (I'm told people have actually *tried* to be named Idiot in past years), the College Idiot (or Idiots, lately) should be somebody, usually a freshman, who a large part of Wiess views as being incurably goofy/dumb/silly. Being elected Idiot's not really a bad thing, per se - if you were an asshole, you would've been elected DWE, instead. Count your blessings." [@riceinfo-pumpkin]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "**College Idiot.** 1. An honorary office conferred upon one deserving Wiessmen each year. The awesome responsibility of donning the Great Pumpkin guise on Halloween night remains the greatest part of this position, but a penchant for general mischief and mayhem are helpful character traits." [@oweek-2003 p.3]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2014"
    "**College Idiot.** An honorary title conferred upon one deserving upperclassman and one freshman each Hallowen." [@oweek-2014 p.102]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2017"
    "**Pumpkin Caroling.** The spreading of Halloween cheer, led by the elected College Idiots. Features Halloween songs and visits to the other colleges." [@oweek-2017 p.15]

## Other named roles

- **Pig Master.** The 1988 Campanile captions the War Pig's builder as "'Pig Master' Jorge Martin-de-Nicolas" [@campanile-1988]. The title does not recur in the corpus; the people who kept the pig after him are "Pig #3's builder" and "the core team" in their own accounts. See [The War Pig](../traditions/warpig.md).
- **DWE.** The Idiot's opposite: the 1999 pumpkin page treats being "elected DWE" as the fate of the college's designated asshole, and records one man elected to both [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. No source in the corpus expands the initials or describes an election.
- **Class Council '06 Historian.** In April 2003 the university-wide Class Council for the class of 2006 posted a one-page Historian Position Application from a temporary home on a Wiess student's machine, jwh06.wiess.rice.edu [@class-council-06-historian]. It is a Class, not a college, office, but it is the earliest written description we hold of a student historian's job at Rice; see [Historians](historians.md).
- **Head Fellows, Fellows, Coordinator.** Not honorary and not covered here: the Fellows are "Peer advisors for new students of Wiess College" [@oweek-2003 p.3]; the Head Fellow enters the Constitution in 2016 [@gov-repo]. See [Governance](../governance/index.md).

## Variants & disputes

- **One Idiot or several.** 1994: "one deserving Wiess member each year (or more…)" [@handbook-1994]. 1999: "(or Idiots, lately)", and the webmaster's own freshman year had two [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. 2003–2011: "one deserving Wiessmen each year" [@oweek-2003 p.3] [@oweek-2011 p.92]. 2014: "one deserving upperclassman and one freshman" [@oweek-2014 p.102]. 2015–17: "the elected College Idiots" [@oweek-2015 p.119]. The books describe the rule of the moment; the practice may have been looser throughout.
- **Elected or conferred.** The glossaries say "conferred" [@handbook-1994]; the tradition pages say "elects" [@oweek-2006 p.43], and the 1999 page uses "elected" and "named" interchangeably [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. Nothing in the corpus describes the mechanism—a Cabinet vote, a caroling-night acclamation, or something else.
- **The prize.** "The monetary prize is not as large as in years past" (1994) [@handbook-1994]; no later source mentions money. In 2015 the Idiot gains a duty instead: "rallying the college with an inspirational speech" [@oweek-2015 p.24].
- **"Usually a freshman."** The 1999 page's rule [@riceinfo-pumpkin] does not fit George Hall, Idiot for four years and President in one of them [@handbook-1994]; by 2014 the books had formalised one of each [@oweek-2014 p.102].

## Open questions

- What "DWE" stands for, and whether it was ever a real election rather than a joke [@riceinfo-pumpkin].
- Whether the Idiot is still elected: the last glossary in the corpus is 2017 [@oweek-2017 p.15], and the current site's Traditions page is not captured in readable form [@wiess-rice-edu].
- Any Idiot before 1979. The Thresher's Halloween coverage of the 1960s–70s might name one.
- Whether the office was ever written into a Constitution or Bylaw: none of the versions in the governance repository mentions it [@gov-repo].
