# Magisters

The Magister[^magister] is a professor who lives next to the college with their family and looks out for it. "Do not let their title mislead you," said the 2003 O-Week book. They're "helpful, loving and supportive people" [@wb 20040619001755 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/01-14%20intro.pdf p.7].

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - The Magister is "the highest-ranking faculty Associate of Wiess College" and can veto Cabinet [@constitution-2026 p.3] [@constitution-1993].
    - The first was Dr. Roy Talmage, in 1957 [@oweek-2006 p.36]. The title was "Master" until 2017 [@oweek-2017 p.20].
    - Since August 2021: Flavio Cunha and Fabiana Santos [@wb 20210813231021 http://teamwiess.com/government/ateam] [@oweek-2025 p.30].
    - Since 2016, a student committee helps pick the Magister [@constitution-2016 p.1].

## Who they've been

The list is solid from about 1990. For earlier years we've found only part of it so far. The full table with sources is in the receipts below.

- **1957–?** Dr. Roy Talmage, "a conservative and strict gentleman" who required "Wiess blazers (complete with crests) for formal Sunday dinners" [@oweek-2006 p.36].
- **1970s.** Lea Rudee, Stephen Baker (acting) and Stewart Baker, known mostly from alumni memories [@rhc 2016-08-16 moving-in-the-rain-1957 comment by Kermit Lancaster, 23 Aug 2016] [@rhc 2013-02-19 dr-baker-rocks comment by Stephen Baker, 19 Feb 2013] [@rhc 2016-03-07 barbara-jordan-1977 comment by Krammit, 7 Mar 2016].
- **c.1990–2001.** John and Paula Hutchinson, who "do a damn fine guitar-vocal duet" [@riceinfo-masters]. The sources disagree on their start: the 1994 handbook has them in "their fifth year," but a 2014 bio says 1994 and seven years [@riceinfo-masters] [@oweek-2014 p.57]. Sources from the time have them in office through 2000 [@riceinfo-news-2000-lundin].
- **2001/02–2006.** Katharine Donato and Dan Kalb [@wb 20040619001755 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/01-14%20intro.pdf p.7].
- **2006–2011.** Mike Gustin and Denise Klein [@oweek-2006 p.8].
- **2011–2016.** Alexander and Jeanette Byrd [@oweek-2011 p.92].
- **2016–2021.** Andrew and Laura Schaefer, both Wiess alums, who met "at (Old) Wiess in 1991" [@oweek-2016 p.19].
- **2021–.** Flavio Cunha and Fabiana Santos [@oweek-2021 p.24] [@oweek-2024 p.29].

Rice Magazine (2016) says George Pharr '75 "became master of Wiess in the early 1990s" [@rice-magazine-2016-wiess-traditions]. That doesn't fit the Hutchinsons' dates [@riceinfo-masters], so he isn't on this list for now.

The Magisters, RAs and Coordinators together: [The Core Team](core-team.md). Their house: [Wilson House](../places/wilson-house.md).

## What they actually do

The 2024 book has the Magisters' own job description: "As magisters, we have four main roles." They advise student government, help with academic advising, support students in emergencies, and work with Housing & Dining on the buildings [@oweek-2024 p.29] [@oweek-2025 p.30].

They're "the middlemen between students and Rice administration" [@oweek-2024 p.28]. Also: "They are always around Wiess eating meals with you, playing volleyball, taking their dogs on walks" [@oweek-2024 p.28]. And go to their teas: "Get to know the masters. You will not regret it" [@wb 20080625214650 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?r=masters].

## How they're picked

At first the administration just appointed them [@constitution-1993]. From 2007, Cabinet recommended [@constitution-2007 p.1]. Since 2016, a committee of at least six Wiess members recommends, and the administration appoints [@constitution-2016 p.1]. Newer versions ask the committee to "reflect the diversity of interests and identities present across Wiess" [@constitution-2026 p.3].

The Magister can veto Cabinet. Since about 2025, Cabinet can answer with "an official statement of dissent by a two-thirds vote" [@constitution-hate-speech Art. VI §5] [@constitution-2026 p.9]. Details: [@gov-repo].

??? info "The receipts: how the rule changed"

    | Version | Rule |
    |---|---|
    | 1993 | The Magister is appointed by the administration [@constitution-1993] |
    | 2007 | "recommended by Cabinet and appointed by the administration" [@constitution-2007 p.1] |
    | 2016 | "recommended by a committee led by two chairmen selected by the President, with the President serving as ex officio. The chairmen shall select no fewer than six Wiess members as members of the committee. The President shall have veto power over membership selections. The Master shall then be appointed by the administration" [@constitution-2016 p.1] |
    | 2020 | Same committee, now for "The Magister" ("led by two Chairmen selected by the President") [@constitution-2020 Art. II §3] |
    | c. 2025, 2026 | Same committee, with the added instruction that members "should seek to reflect the diversity of interests and identities present across Wiess" [@constitution-hate-speech Art. II §1] [@constitution-2026 p.3] |
