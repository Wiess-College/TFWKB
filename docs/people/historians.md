# Historians

Wiess already has a Historian, and has had one on paper for at least thirty-five years. The 1991 Rules charge "The Historian of the Cabinet" with keeping the Rules up to date and available [@rules-1991]; the 1993 Constitution created "a College Parliamentarian and Historian who shall serve the Cabinet in a non-voting capacity", and every Constitution through 2017 repeats the sentence word for word [@constitution-1993] [@constitution-2017 p.5]. The O-Week books name a Cabinet Historian in every year they describe the Cabinet, from 2003 to 2017, with the same job each time: "organizes all of the pictures taken by Wiessmen and keeps records of all the random stuff we do for historical reference" [@oweek-2007 p.39]; the 2023 Cabinet had two [@wb 20230915232631 https://wiess.rice.edu/government/cabinet]. The 2026 Constitution is the first to spell the duties out: "official Historian(s) of the College" who are "charged with recording, archiving, and presenting the history of the College" and with running the college's Facebook and Instagram [@constitution-2026 p.8]. What the record does not show is any of the output. No photograph archive, record book or history produced by a Cabinet Historian is in the corpus or named in any source; the institutional memory that survives was made by a Resident Associate, Dr. Bill Wilson, and now sits in the Woodson Research Center [@rhc 2021-11-15 secession-1992]. The question for anyone deciding about the office is therefore not whether to create it but what it should leave behind.

    "The Historian of the Cabinet shall take care that an up-to-date copy of the rules are maintained by him/herself, the Wiess College President, the Court, and the Master." [@rules-1991]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "Historian: Abby Watrous is in charge of keeping Wiess records and memories for future generations of Wiessmen." [@wb 20040416083807 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/35-44%20wiess.pdf p.4]

    "There shall be official Historian(s) of the College who shall… Be charged with recording, archiving, and presenting the history of the College." [@constitution-2026 p.8]

## What was actually kept

The sources agree on who the college's de-facto archivist was. Dr. Bill Wilson, RA for some thirty years, was "taking pictures of almost every Wiess event" by 1997 [@riceinfo-associates], "recording things on campus" in 2003 [@oweek-2003 p.3], and by 2014 had a "section in Fondren Library [that] includes every college theatre production during his time at Rice" [@oweek-2014 p.102]. The 2006 Historian's job was defined as organising *his* pictures [@oweek-2006 p.39]. The Rice archivist found "hundreds of photographs that were taken by Dr. Bill Wilson" in the Wiess College collection [@rhc 2014-01-24 friday-afternoon-follies-dr-bill-in-a-skirt] and, in 2021, "hundreds of recordings of Rice events made by Dr. Bill" among his papers [@rhc 2021-11-15 secession-1992]. The Wiess College Records at the Woodson (UA 0079) hold Cabinet minutes and governing documents from 1950 [@woodson-ua0079]. See [Resident Associates](resident-associates.md).

The student record is the O-Week books, which the Head Fellows write; the websites, which the webmasters built; and the glossaries—see [How we described ourselves, by year](../traditions/glossary-series.md) and [The Wiess website through the years](../web/the-wiess-website-through-the-years.md). None of it is attributed to a Historian.

## Variants & disputes

- **"Nothing in the constitutions."** The working assumption when this site was planned was that no Wiess constitution mentioned a historian. Every tagged version in the governance repository does [@gov-repo]: as a non-voting Parliamentarian-and-Historian pair from 1993 to 2017, and as a defined office in 2026. The 1991 Rules are older still [@rules-1991].
- **One office or two.** The 1993–2017 sentence can be read as one person ("a College Parliamentarian and Historian") or two [@constitution-1993]. The O-Week books always list them separately [@oweek-2007 p.39], and the 2026 text separates them [@constitution-2026 p.8]. Two offices, awkwardly drafted.
- **Appointed how.** 1993–2017: "in the same manner as the selection of the appointed Cabinet members"—in 1993 "selected by the Cabinet in the spring semester" [@constitution-1993], in 2016 "selected by the President with the approval of Cabinet" [@constitution-2016 p.2], and from 29 March 2017 "selected by the Cabinet" again [@constitution-2017 p.2]; 2026: "appointed by the President with the assent of Cabinet" after an independent application [@constitution-2026 p.8]. The governance repository's diffs show the sequence [@gov-repo].
- **What the job is.** "keeping Wiess records and memories" (2003); organising Dr. Bill's photographs (2006); organising everyone's photographs and keeping records (2007–14); also taking the photographs (2015–17); "recording, archiving, and presenting" plus social media (2026). The photographic side has grown while the records side has stayed a phrase.
- **The Class Council application.** The 2003 Class Council '06 Historian application [@class-council-06-historian] is often the first thing found when searching for a Rice student historian role; it belongs to a university-wide class office and not to Wiess, whose own Historian was already in the 2003 O-Week book.

## Open questions

- **Where is the output?** No photo archive, record book, Facebook album or "history" produced by any Historian 2003–2023 is in the corpus. The 2012 "Wiess Historian" Facebook account [@wb 20120914230516 http://teamwiess.com/about.php] and the Instagram the 2026 Constitution assigns [@constitution-2026 p.8] are the places to look; neither is archived by the Wayback Machine in a readable form.
- **Did a Historian ever deposit anything with the Woodson?** The Wiess College Records finding aid [@woodson-ua0079] would show whether student-kept material arrived there.
- **Historians before 2003.** The 1993 Constitution and 1991 Rules create the office [@constitution-1993] [@rules-1991]; no source names a holder before Abby Watrous in 2003. Cabinet minutes in the Woodson would.
- **The 1991 Rules' Historian** kept the Rules; nothing says who kept the Constitution's text, which this site's governance repository now reconstructs from web captures with gaps of fourteen and nine years [@gov-repo].
- **2018–2022 holders**, between the last O-Week book in the corpus and the 2023 Cabinet page.
