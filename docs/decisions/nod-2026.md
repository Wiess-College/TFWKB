# NOD 2026

**Status:** stub — to be written by the people who made the decision · **Date:** unknown

This page is a placeholder for a decision record about Night of Decadence in 2026. The corpus this site is built from holds nothing on any 2025–26 decision about NOD: the only NOD-specific URL on the current website is a 512-byte capture of wiess.rice.edu/nod/ from 23 January 2025, whose content did not survive [@wiess-rice-edu-nod-2025]; the "Complete Guiding Documents" of 23 February 2026 do not mention Night of Decadence at all [@constitution-2026]; and no O-Week book after 2017 is in the corpus. Nothing below should be read as a statement of what was decided. The headings are the standard ones from the [Decisions](index.md) page; the notes under each say what the record needs.

## Context

*To be written.* What prompted the decision — a University or Student Judicial Programs requirement, a Cabinet or Social Vice Presidents' proposal, a cost, an incident, a survey of the college. Cite the Cabinet minutes or the announcement to the College.

What the record can already point to as background: NOD has been held at Wiess since 1972 and described to freshmen as "the biggest party at Rice" in every glossary from 2006 to 2014, held "on the last Friday of October" (2003–08), "the last Saturday of October" (2010–16) and "in October" (2017) [@rice-magazine-2016-wiess-traditions] [@oweek-2006 p.84] [@oweek-2010 p.91] [@oweek-2017 p.15]. The 2026 Constitution gives the Social Vice Presidents the duty to "Plan and direct the social activities of the College including the public parties" [@constitution-2026 Art. V §1 (4)]. See [Night of Decadence](../traditions/night-of-decadence.md) for the history.
