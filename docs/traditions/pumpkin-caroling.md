# Pumpkin Caroling

Around Halloween each year Wiess elects a [College Idiot](../people/college-idiot.md), crowns them with a pumpkin as the Great Pumpkin, and parades the campus by candlelight singing Christmas carols with Halloween words — "Great Pumpkin is Coming to Town", "Deck the Patch", "I'm Dreaming of the Great Pumpkin" — at the other colleges, at Willy's statue (where a candle-lit pumpkin goes on Willy's head), through Fondren Library "quiet like mice", and to the President's house. The tradition is older than its Wiess form: the Thresher reported Baker students pumpkin caroling in 1965 and 1966, and a Rice archives photograph shows Wiess carolers with candles at Halloween 1969. Most of the lyrics were not Wiess's own. In December 1999 the college's webmaster posted a note headed "We Goofed": a publishing offer had revealed that the verses "were borrowed way back from Peanuts creator Charles Schulz", who had published them "in a set of Halloween greeting cards around 1969". The Idiot has gone from one honorary office (1994) to "Idiots, lately" (1997), to "one deserving upperclassman and one freshman" (2014), to "the elected College Idiots" (2015–17). The college site was still posting pumpkin-caroling photographs in 2023 and 2025.

## As the college described it

!!! quote "1994 Freshman Handbook"
    "Pumpkin Caroling! — Wiess' spreading of good Halloween cheer. Favorite songs include 'You Better Watch Out' and 'I'm Dreaming of the Great Pumpkin.' (...with every pumpkin card I write...)" [@handbook-1994]

!!! quote "David Cunningham, riceinfo site, August 1997 — the Idiot"
    "A highly-prized honor here at Wiess (I'm told people have actually *tried* to be named Idiot in past years), the College Idiot (or Idiots, lately) should be somebody, usually a freshman, who a large part of Wiess views as being incurably goofy/dumb/silly. Being elected Idiot's not really a bad thing, per se - if you were an asshole, you would've been elected DWE, instead. Count your blessings." [@riceinfo-pumpkin]

!!! quote "The same page — the route"
    "Wiess (obviously) - crown the Idiot(s) with their own personal pumpkins, making them like unto the image of the Great Pumpkin himself. (And please don't let the Idiot(s) run into any trees.) … Willy's Statue - the most important part of the ceremony. Get some agile Wiessman to climb up and place a pumpkin with a candle in it on Willy's head, then prostrate yourselves before the Idiot(s) and sing! Sing! … Fondren - file in quiet like mice and sing to all the brain-fried grad students. Then yell and scream on the way out. … Gillis' House - and maybe you can even get him to come along! (Rupp never would. We scared the hell out of him.) … BrownJones - that horrifying composite beast on the far side of the campus. Just call it 'Brones,' and please everybody. … Back to Wiess, triumphant and joyful - go eat cookies at Wiess House." [@riceinfo-pumpkin]

!!! quote "Ray Wagner, 'We Goofed', 29 December 1999"
    "It seems that there has been quite an interest lately in these pumpkin carols. As a result of a publishing offer from the Golden Books Publishing Co., we learned that these carols are not entirely ours. It appears that some of the lyrics were borrowed way back from Peanuts creator Charles Schulz. He published the borrowed verses in a set of Halloween greeting cards around 1969. We'd like to thank Mr. Schulz for the many years his carols have brought Halloween joy to Rice University and Wiess College and give him the credit he is certainly due. - RSW, 12-29-99" [@riceinfo-pumpkin] On the homepage: "It seems that those crazy Pumpkin Carols we've been singing all these years weren't entirely Wiess originals. Get the dirt from the Pumpkin Carols page." [@wb 20000818090739 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "Been wondering what to do on Halloween now that you're too old to go Trick or Treating? Never fear, Wiess has the answer: pumpkin caroling. Every Halloween time, Wiess elects a College Idiot, who wins the honor to serve as the Great Pumpkin. Then, with candles and song lyrics in hand, we parade around campus singing caroling melodies with adjusted lyrics at the top of our lungs." [@wb 20040416083807 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/35-44%20wiess.pdf p.8 (printed 42)]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2015"
    "Every Halloween, Wiess elects a College Idiot, who wins the honor of rallying the college with an inspirational speech and serving as the Great Pumpkin. Then, with candles, song lyrics, and tasty fall treats in hand, we parade around campus singing caroling melodies with modified lyrics. The entire event is magnificent, but it gets even better when we visit the library." [@oweek-2015 p.24]

!!! quote "Wiess Associates, 'Wiess Traditions', c.2017"
    "Every Halloween, Wiess elects a College Idiot, who wins the honor of rallying the college with an inspirational speech and serving as the Great Pumpkin. Then, with candles, song lyrics, and tasty fall treats in hand, we parade around campus singing caroling melodies with modified lyrics." ## Variants & disputes

**Whose carols?** The 1994 handbook and the 1997 page present the songs as the college's ("The Official Wiess College Pumpkin Carols") [@handbook-1994] [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. The college itself corrected this in December 1999: the verses came from Charles Schulz's Halloween cards of c.1969 [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. Rice History Corner's 2016 post reproduces a Schulz Museum image of the carol cards and dates the fad to the 1966 television special [@rhc 2016-10-28 friday-follies-halloween-1969]. Which of the nine carols on the 1997 page are Schulz's and which are Rice's (the "We the Clowns of Wiess College" verse, at least, is local) has not been worked out; the 1999 note says "some of the lyrics".

**How old is it, and whose was it first?** The Thresher of 3 November 1966 describes a Baker revival in 1965 and a Baker-led troupe in 1966 that sang *at* Wiess, "which ignored them" [@thresher-1966-11-03]. By Halloween 1969 Wiess students were photographed caroling with candles [@rice-archives-pumpkin-caroling-1969]. So pumpkin caroling at Rice predates Wiess's version, and Wiess's version is at least as old as 1969 — older than any date the college's own books give, which give none. Whether Wiess took it over from Baker, or ran its own from 1967–69, is not in the record. The 1997 page's "I'm told people have actually *tried* to be named Idiot in past years" and the 1994 "monetary prize … not as large as in years past" both imply an office already old by the early 1990s [@riceinfo-pumpkin] [@handbook-1994].

**One Idiot or several.** "One deserving Wiess member each year (or more …)" in 1994 [@handbook-1994]; "the College Idiot (or Idiots, lately) … usually a freshman" in 1997 [@riceinfo-pumpkin]; "one deserving Wiessmen each year" in the 2003–2010 glossaries [@oweek-2003 p.3] [@oweek-2010 p.90]; "one deserving upperclassman and one freshman each Hallowen" in 2014 [@oweek-2014 p.102]; "the elected College Idiots" from 2015, by which time the glossary had dropped the office's own entry [@oweek-2015 p.119]. The 1994 handbook also records George Hall as Idiot for four successive years, 1979–82, so the "one each year" rule was never strict [@handbook-1994]. See [College Idiot & other offices](../people/college-idiot.md).

**Who organises it.** The Executive Vice President in 1998 [@wb 19990219085339 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/people/cabinet.html]; the Internal Vice President in 2003 [@wb 20040416083807 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/35-44%20wiess.pdf p.4 (printed 38)]. The 2026 Constitution gives the Internal VP Summit and Big Bang and does not mention caroling [@constitution-2026]; whether it moved to another officer or simply went unwritten is unknown.

**The President's house.** Cunningham's route includes "Gillis' House - and maybe you can even get him to come along! (Rupp never would.)" [@riceinfo-pumpkin], and the 2003 glossary lists "a visit to the Gillis home" [@oweek-2003 p.4]. From 2006 the glossary says "a visit to the other colleges" [@oweek-2006 p.84] — the sentence was updated when Gillis left, and no later president is named. Whether the carolers still call at the President's house is not in the corpus.

**DWE.** The 1997 page's foil to the Idiot — "if you were an asshole, you would've been elected DWE, instead" — is never expanded anywhere in the corpus [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. It is not in any glossary.

**Who wrote the 1997 page.** The route and the Idiot section are dated 8/11/97 by David Cunningham in the January 1999 capture [@wb 19990117034518 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/pumpkin.html]; Ray Wagner's 29 Dec 1999 revision added only the Schulz note and his byline [@riceinfo-pumpkin]. The writer's "Back when I was a Freshman" and his talk of Gillis caroling place his first year in the Gillis presidency. The page named four past Idiots; this site does not repeat the pairing of one of them with the DWE title.

## Open questions

- The Thresher, 1967–1990: when does "pumpkin caroling" first appear as a Wiess event, and when does the "College Idiot" first appear in print? The Portal's OCR search for both phrases has not been logged.
- The 1969 lyric sheets scanned on Rice History Corner [@rhc 2019-10-30 im-dreaming-of-the-great-pumpkin-1969] — are they Wiess's, Baker's or Schulz's cards? Comparing them with the 1997 page's nine carols would show what Wiess added.
- A list of College Idiots by year: none exists in the corpus. The 1994 handbook (Hall, 1979–82) and the 1997 page (four names, c.1993–96) are the only sources; Cabinet minutes in Woodson (UA 0079) would have the elections.
- Does the route still include Willy's statue, Fondren and the President's house? The last route description is 1997; the 2015 book mentions only "the library" [@oweek-2015 p.24].
- The 2023 and 2025 images on wiess.rice.edu are known only by file name; what they show, and the page text around them, needs a browser.
