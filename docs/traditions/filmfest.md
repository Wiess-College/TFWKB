# FilmFest

FilmFest was Wiess's 24-hour movie marathon at the end of classes, before finals: first in the Old Wiess basement, later in the New Wiess movie room [@oweek-2015 p.23] [@handbook-1994]. The latest book we've found that mentions it is 2017's [@oweek-2017 p.14].

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - It's in the 1994 handbook, in the Old Wiess basement [@handbook-1994].
    - In New Wiess it moved to the fourth-floor TV room, later the "legendary movie room" [@oweek-2003 p.4] [@oweek-2015 p.23].
    - The latest book we've found that mentions it is 2017's. The 2019–2025 books don't bring it up [@oweek-2017 p.14] [@oweek-2019 p.30].

## How it worked

Classes are over. Finals are coming. Obviously, the answer is 24 straight hours of movies. Movies from every genre, "from midnight to midnight" [@oweek-2015 p.23]. The 1997 website put it best: "24 *solid* hours of movies. Caffeine is good for the soul" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997].

How often depends on who you ask. The books say once a semester in 1994 and 2017, but the 1994 webmaster didn't recall it "ever happening more than once a year" [@handbook-1994] [@oweek-2017 p.14].

The books sold it honestly. It's "The best way to waste time when you should be studying" (2003) [@oweek-2003 p.4]. It's "the perfect way to take a study break or to procrastinate—whichever you prefer" (2015) [@oweek-2015 p.23].

## The room

In 1994 it ran in the Wiess basement [@handbook-1994]. In New Wiess, the TV room "stadium seats 35 people and is blacked out for the optimum viewing experience at times such at 2 PM" [@oweek-2006 p.42].

By 2015 it was the movie room, "with countless couches, a state of the art projector, and blacked out windows" [@oweek-2015 p.23]. Recent books still brag about the room, but as a place to rewatch Game of Thrones, not for a marathon [@oweek-2019 p.30] [@oweek-2025 p.11].

## Before FilmFest

Wiess has loved movies for a long time. In 1965 it started "a yearlong film festival specializing in 'classic revivals'" [@thresher-1965-09-30-film-festival]. That was a film series, though, not a 24-hour marathon.

??? quote "In the college's own words"
    "**Filmfest**—Twenty-four-hour movie marathon in the Wiess Basement. Generally occurs once a semester. (That's what the book says. I don't recall it ever happening more than once a year, myself...)" [@handbook-1994]

    "Classes are over and finals are fast approaching. How does Wiess make sure you prepare for your finals? That's easy – a twenty-four hour extravaganza of movies." [@oweek-2006 p.42]

    "Then there's the surround sound … let's just say you can feel the room shake at times." [@oweek-2006 p.42]

    The glossary: "A 24-hour film marathon held during Dead Week" (2003–2011) [@oweek-2003 p.4] [@oweek-2011 p.93]; "held during Dead Days" (2014–2016) [@oweek-2014 p.102] [@oweek-2016 p.13]; "held once a semester" (2017) [@oweek-2017 p.14].
