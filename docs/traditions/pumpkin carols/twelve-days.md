# The Twelve Days of Halloween

On the 1st day of Halloween
My true love gave to me
An owl in a rotten oak tree
[short pause] 

On the 2nd day of Halloween
My true love gave to me
Two trick-or-treaters
An owl in a rotten oak tree
[short pause]

On the 3rd day of Halloween
My true love gave to me
Three black cats
Two trick-or-treaters
An owl in a rotten oak tree
[short pause]

On the 4th day of Halloween
My true love gave to me
Four skeletons
