# We the Clowns of Wiess College  
  
We the clowns of Wiess College are  
Singing tunes we learned in a bar  
Of dark demons, ghosts and goblins  
Great Pumpkin's our shining star  
Ohhhhhhh, ohhhhhhh Star of horror, star of blight  
Star with mystic powers if might  
Upward leading we are pleading  
Let this be Great Pumpkin's night
