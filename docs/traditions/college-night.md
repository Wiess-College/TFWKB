# College Night

College Night is the end-of-semester celebration every Rice college holds; Wiess's version was, for most of the record, distinguished by its date and its second act. It fell on the last day of classes each semester — "Always held the last day of classes" in every glossary from 2003 to 2014 — and followed a dressy dinner with "Entertainment", a programme of music, skits and the freshmen's own Freshman Song [@oweek-2003 p.3] [@riceinfo-collegenight]. The 1997 website put "College Night (and Entertainment)" among the traditions "Older Than Time" and asked of the other colleges, "Entertainment at their College Nights? Nope" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]. By 2015 the books describe a themed day "typically on Fridays" with a fancy dinner and "a night full of celebrations", and the entertainment programme is no longer mentioned [@oweek-2015 p.24]. The 2026 Constitution provides for "At least 2 College Night Representatives" to run the fall and spring nights [@constitution-2026 p.10], and the current site published College Night photographs in 2023 and 2024 [@wiess-rice-edu asset-manifest.json].

## As the college described it

!!! quote "riceinfo site, 'Eat, Drink, and Be Merry', 23 Jun 1999"
    "Everybody has 'em, and they seem to enjoy coming up with 'fun' themes for each one - which isn't necessarily bad, but Wiess' College Night is better. It's held every semester, on the last day of classes, and starts out with a dinner, very nice'n'polite, so we Wiessmen can demonstrate just how smooth we can be in nice clothes. Afterwards is the fun part, usually called 'Entertainment.' It used to be held in the Wiess Commons right after dinner, but moving everything around and cleaning everything up got to be a pain in the ass… Lots of musical numbers, it seems like, from quiet guitar to loud punk bands to sing-along country. Freshmen each year come up with their very own Freshman Song." [@riceinfo-collegenight]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "Each college has one night of the semester where they play all afternoon, have a fancy dinner and bond all night. Wisely, Wiess puts theirs on the last night of the semester to make things extra special… Dinner is dressy, complete with a theme, tablecloths and 1,000 cheesesticks (you think it's a lot, but you better get there early). After dinner, we all get together for performances, a senior slide show in the spring and more fun times." [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9] (the same text 2006–2011 [@oweek-2006 p.44] [@oweek-2011 p.46])

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2015"
    "College Night is an event held by each residential college once per semester, typically on Fridays. They're characterized by fun all afternoon, a fancy dinner, and a night full of celebrations. Each College Night has its own new and exciting theme… It's one of the highlights of the semester, so stay tuned for a theme announcement!" [@oweek-2015 p.24] (unchanged 2016, 2017 [@oweek-2016 p.32] [@oweek-2017 p.33])

## Variants & disputes

- **Themes.** In 1999 the themes were what *other* colleges did ("they seem to enjoy coming up with 'fun' themes… but Wiess' College Night is better") [@riceinfo-collegenight]; from 2003 the dinner is "complete with a theme" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9] and from 2015 the theme is the headline [@oweek-2015 p.24]. This may be what the 1997 site's "No-Theme Wiess", a tradition "Sadly Seem[ing] To Be Dying Out", referred to — see [No-Theme Wiess](retired/no-theme-wiess.md) — but no source connects the two.
- **The date.** "Last day of classes" in 1999 and in every book to 2014 [@riceinfo-collegenight] [@oweek-2014 p.102]; "typically on Fridays" and no mention of the last day from 2015 [@oweek-2015 p.24]. The 2014 glossary's insistence that other colleges' nights "are not always on the last day of classes" reads as the last defence of the old rule.
- **Entertainment.** A named second half with a Freshman Song in 1999 [@riceinfo-collegenight]; "performances, a senior slide show in the spring" to 2011 [@oweek-2011 p.46]; "a night of bonding, pictures, and memories" in 2014 [@oweek-2014 p.43]; "a night full of celebrations" from 2015 [@oweek-2015 p.24]. Whether the Freshman Song survived past 1999 is not recorded.
- **Afternoon.** Not in the 1999 account; "play all afternoon" from 2003 [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9]; "a full day of themed partying" in 2016 [@owlmanac-2016 p.55].

## Open questions

- When College Night began at Wiess; "Older Than Time" in 1997 is the earliest word [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]. Earlier handbooks and the Cabinet minutes are the places to look.
- Any Freshman Song lyrics. Wagner asked for "this year's" in 1999 and never posted them [@riceinfo-collegenight].
- When College Night Representatives were created: present by April 2017 [@wb 20170421224901 http://teamwiess.com/profiles/reps/collegenightreps.jpg], written into the Constitution by 2026 [@constitution-2026 p.10]; the 2013–2017 constitutions in the governance repo do not mention them [@gov-repo].
- The 1,000 cheesesticks: in every book 2003–2014 and still unexplained.
