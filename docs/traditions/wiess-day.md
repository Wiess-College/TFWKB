# Wiess Day

Wiess Day is a spring day in the Acabowl—food, games and whatever the Social Vice Presidents think of—that the college dates to the spring of 2008: "a new tradition began… and it was named Wiess Day!" says the O-Week book written that summer [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8]. The first one had "a slip-n-slide, food fight, tug-of-war, eating contest"; later years added "300 lbs of crawfish, an ice-cream truck, and a petting zoo" [@oweek-2014 p.43]. It began alongside [JamFest](jamfest.md), the older spring concert, shared a glossary entry with it in 2010–11 as "Jamfest/Wiess Day", and outlived it: by 2014 "Wiess Day has become an annual tradition… and things like Jamfest have come and gone" [@oweek-2010 p.91] [@oweek-2014 p.37]. The 2015–2017 books claim it as unique—"only Wiess has Wiess Day" [@oweek-2015 p.24]. Nothing after 2017 is in the corpus.

## As the college described it

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2008"
    "In the Spring of 2008, a new tradition began... and it was named Wiess Day! Our amazing socials decided to throw a party in the Acabowl complete with games and food. Wiessmen celebrated with first annual Wiess day with a slip-n-slide, food fight, tug-of-war, eating contest, and other fun in the sun!" [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8]

!!! quote "teamwiess.com, 2014–15"
    "In the spring of 2008, a new tradition began: Wiess Day! Our socials had a brilliant idea to throw a party in the Acabowl complete with games, food, and fun. Wiessmen celebrated the first annual Wiess day with a slip-n-slide, food fight, tug-of-war, eating contest, and other fun in the sun. Since then, we have made great additions like 300 lbs of crawfish, an ice-cream truck, and a petting zoo!" [@wb 20150313224552 http://teamwiess.com/traditions.html]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2015"
    "While every residential college can celebrate with a College Night, only Wiess has Wiess Day. This is a relatively new tradition, originating in the spring of 2008. This day of celebration centers around the fact that we're from Wiess and Wiess is great. Wiess Day is planned by the Social Vice Presidents, so most of the activities are up to their discretion, but past years' have included a slip-n-slide, an ice cream truck, and a petting zoo (complete with goats and a very small horse). Nobody knows what Wiess Day will hold, but it will surely be a day to remember." [@oweek-2015 p.24]

## Variants & disputes

- **New tradition, or JamFest renamed?** Two readings exist on this site. The traditions index takes the glossary sequence—JamFest 2003–08, "Jamfest/Wiess Day" 2010–11, Wiess Day alone from 2014—as the stronger evidence and calls the 2015 website's "a new tradition began" a rebranding remembered as a founding. The 2008 book, the earliest source and written the summer after the first Wiess Day, supports the website: it describes Wiess Day as a new Socials' party *and* JamFest as a continuing concert, on facing pages [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8] [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.9]. The 2010 and 2011 books likewise carry separate Wiess Day and Jamfest essays while their glossaries fuse the two [@oweek-2010 p.44] [@oweek-2010 p.45] [@oweek-2010 p.91]. The better description is therefore: Wiess Day was founded in spring 2008 as a new event, ran beside JamFest for at least three springs, and had absorbed it by 2014 [@oweek-2014 p.37]. The "Jamfest/Wiess Day" glossary line records the overlap, not a rename.
- **Whose idea.** "Our amazing socials decided" (2008) [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8]; "Our socials had a brilliant idea" (2014) [@oweek-2014 p.43]; "planned by the Social Vice Presidents" (2015) [@oweek-2015 p.24]. No individual is credited in any source.
- **The copy-paste page.** The June 2014 website gave "Jazz Night" the Wiess Day paragraph by mistake; fixed by March 2015 [@teamwiess-traditions-2014].

- Who the Social Vice Presidents of 2007–08 were; the 2008 book's Cabinet page should name them [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.4].
- When the petting zoo first came (between 2011 and 2014) and whether the crawfish boil is a Wiess Day fixture or a separate themed dinner (the 2006 book mentions "a N'Awlins crawfish boil compliments of our Culturals" as a themed dinner [@oweek-2006 p.42]).
- Whether Wiess Day continues after 2017; the current site's traditions page has not been captured [@wiess-rice-edu].
