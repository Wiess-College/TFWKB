# Freshman Weenie Roast

The Freshman Weenie Roast was an all-college cookout thrown by the freshmen: two of them, the "Weenie Reps", were "handpicked by a committee of former Weenie Reps" and had to raise the money, plan it and publicise it "which may include dancing, posters, songs or whatever else the Weenies dream up" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.8]. It is on the 1997 website's list of traditions "We'd Sure Like To See Continue" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] and is described in the 2003, 2006 and 2007 O-Week books in the same words [@oweek-2007 p.43]. The 2008 book drops it and no later source mentions it, so it stopped — or stopped being told to freshmen — between the summers of 2007 and 2008. It never had a glossary entry.

## As the college described it

!!! quote "riceinfo site, 1997"
    "Freshman Weenie Roast" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] — a name on a list, nothing more.

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "Two freshmen, handpicked by a committee of former Weenie Reps, are given the responsibility of throwing an all-college Weenie Roast. The reps must collect money for the roast and plan it. Most importantly, they have to publicize, which may include dancing, posters, songs or whatever else the Weenies dream up." [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.8] (identical in 2006 and 2007 [@oweek-2006 p.43] [@oweek-2007 p.43])

## Variants & disputes

- **Freshman or Weenie.** The 1997 site calls it the "Freshman Weenie Roast" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]; the books call it "The Weenie Roast" and the organisers "Weenie Reps" and "the Weenies" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.8]. Same event; this site keeps the older name.
- **Not a Cabinet office.** The Weenie Reps are not among the representatives in the 2003 book's Cabinet page or in any constitution [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.4] [@gov-repo]; they were a self-perpetuating committee of former reps, which is probably why the tradition could lapse without a rule changing.

## Open questions

- When it began. 1997 is the first mention and gives no date [@riceinfo-traditions-1997].
- Whether a Weenie Roast was held in fall 2007 or 2008, and why it stopped. The 2007–08 Cabinet minutes on teamwiess.com (index.php?r=lastnotes) might record it.
- Whether the "all-college" cookout at Beer Bike or Wiess Day (crawfish, ice-cream truck) absorbed its function — see [Wiess Day](../wiess-day.md).
