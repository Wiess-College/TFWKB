# JamFest

JamFest was Wiess's spring music festival: a day of live bands in the [Acabowl](../../places/acabowl.md), usually on a Friday in mid-April [@oweek-2003 p.4] [@portal metapth246567 p.15]. It began in 1987 as a talent show in the [Commons](../../places/commons.md), grew into an all-day concert with touring headliners in the 2000s, and gave way to [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md) around 2010 [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest] [@oweek-2010 p.91] [@oweek-2014 p.37].

<div class="facts" markdown>

- **Held** Spring, usually a Friday in mid-April
- **Place** Commons (1987–88), Acabowl (from 1993)
- **First found** March 1987
- **Last found** April 2011
- **Followed by** [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md)

</div>

## How it worked

Student bands from Rice played alongside Houston bands. From about 2000, a touring band headlined [@portal metapth246676 p.19] [@wb 20070206202205 http://www.teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm]. By 2008, bands auditioned for a slot [@wb 20090917020034 http://teamwiess.com/activities.php].

At its largest, from 2005 to 2007, the show ran from the afternoon until 2 a.m. with 12 to 14 acts. In 2005 there were two stages [@wb 20070206202123 http://www.teamwiess.com/Jamfest/playlist.htm] [@portal metapth443062 p.9] [@wb 20070709183131 http://teamwiess.com/Jamfest/bands.htm].

The date usually fell on Owl Weekend, when admitted students visit Rice [@oweek-2003 p.4].

[Dr. Bill Wilson](../../people/associates.md), a longtime Wiess resident associate, ran the sound and lent students his equipment [@portal metapth443181 p.1] [@portal metapth443018 p.6]. In 2007 a [Dr. Bill Wilson Student Initiative Grant](https://drbillgrant.rice.edu/recipient-history/) paid for "Wiess JamFest Audio Equipment".

## History

```timeline
from: 1985
to: 2023
Talent show in the Commons: ?1987-1992
Concert in the Acabowl: 1993-2001
All-day festival with headliners: 2002-2009
Short evening show: 2010-2011?
Wiess Day (separate party): 2008-2021?
* Two stages, 14 acts: 2005
* JamFest line in the budget: 2014
```

**Talent show, 1987–1992.** The first JamFest found so far was "an open musical talent show, in the Commons" on 27 March 1987 [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest]. It was a talent show again in 1988 [@portal metapth245688 p.20]. In 1992 the ads read "JAMFEST RETURNS" and asked for bands [@portal metapth245802 p.24] [@portal metapth245808 p.20].

**Acabowl concert, 1993–2001.** In 1993 JamFest moved outdoors to the Acabowl [@portal metapth245840 p.20]. By 1997 the college described it as "an annual party/concert held at Wiess every spring, featuring lots of Wiess/Rice/Houston bands" [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html]. Some years had a theme: "Acapalooza" (1997), "Jambalayafest" (2001) and "JAMaica me crazy FEST" (2002) [@portal metapth246567 p.15] [@portal metapth443074 p.31] [@portal metapth443185 p.19].

**All-day festival, 2002–2009.** Bowling for Soup headlined in 2002 and 2003 [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9] [@portal metapth398462 p.9]. The 2003 O-Week book called it "an all-day festival" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9]. A 2004 Thresher op-ed called it "poorly attended"; a 2006 editorial pointed to its "popularity" [@portal metapth398440 p.3] [@portal metapth443047 p.2].

**Wiess Day, 2008 on.** [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md) began in spring 2008 as a separate party [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8]. In 2010 JamFest ran three hours with three Rice bands, and the O-Week glossary listed the two together as "Jamfest/Wiess Day" [@portal metapth398481 p.15] [@oweek-2010 p.91]. The 2011 show added comedy [@portal metapth398487 p.15]. The 2014–15 budget still had a JamFest line [@budget-2014-15 p.1].

A 2006 Thresher feature called that year's show the 38th JamFest, which would date the first to 1968 [@portal metapth443062 p.9]. The earliest found so far is 1987.
