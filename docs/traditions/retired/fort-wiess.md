# Fort Wiess

Fort Wiess was a wooden structure Wiess built for the [Beer Bike](beer-bike.md) parade in its last springs at Old Wiess, around 2001–02 [@thresher-2002-04-05 p.6] [@thresher-2001-04-06 p.29].

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - The 2002 Thresher photos show the inflatable "battle sow" flying over "the Wiess fort" [@thresher-2002-04-05 p.6].
    - That's most of what we've found so far. It isn't in the college websites or O-Week books we have.
    - It matters because it fixes a mix-up: the first wooden War Pig came in 2012, not 2002 [@campanile-2012 p.172].

## What we've found

Before the wooden pig, there was a wooden fort. On 5 April 2002, the Thresher printed Beer Bike photos: "Team Wiess's battle sow flying high in the sky," over the Wiess fort [@thresher-2002-04-05 p.6]. The pig was a balloon. The fort was on the ground.

We haven't found it in the college's own writing yet. The 2003 O-Week history doesn't mention it [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.3].

## Why it matters

Some alumni remember a wooden pig from the Old Wiess days. They're probably remembering the fort, with the balloon above it. The first wooden pig was built by the Class of 2012 [@campanile-2012 p.172]. See [The War Pig](warpig.md).
