# Unnecessary Tie Day

Unnecessary Tie Day was exactly what it says. "This one's pretty self-explanatory. Wear a tie. What could be simpler? A little bit of randomness can be fun," wrote Ray Wagner on the page he gave it in June 1999, illustrated with a borrowed Elvis tie [@riceinfo-tieday]. Two years earlier David Cunningham had listed it, with an exclamation mark, among the traditions "We'd Sure Like To See Continue" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]; six months after writing the page, Wagner moved it to "Traditions That Sadly Seem To Be Dying Out" [@riceinfo-traditions-1999]. Nothing after December 1999 mentions it. When it fell, who picked the day, and whether it was one day a year or any day somebody called it, the record does not say.

## Variants & disputes

- **Continue, or dying.** The same website wanted it to continue in 1997 and called it dying in December 1999 [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@riceinfo-traditions-1999]. The second judgement is Wagner's, who had just written the page, so it is a report, not a slip.

## Open questions

- When it was held and who declared it; the page does not say [@riceinfo-tieday].
- Whether any later class revived it. The O-Week books have no trace, but a one-day joke would not necessarily reach a glossary.
- The `elvistie` image on the page is in the riceinfo mirror and has not been looked at [@riceinfo-site].
