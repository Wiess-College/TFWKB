# No-Theme Wiess

No-Theme Wiess is three words on two lists. On 11 August 1997 it was the last item under "Traditions That Sadly Seem To Be Dying Out" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]; on 30 December 1999 Ray Wagner moved it up to "Traditions We'd Sure Like To See Continue"—the only tradition promoted in his revision, while two others were demoted [@riceinfo-traditions-1999]. It is never explained. The likeliest reading is that it names a refusal: at a university where every public party and every College Night had a theme, Wiess's did not. The same site's College Night page, written in the same year, makes the point about other colleges—"they seem to enjoy coming up with 'fun' themes for each one - which isn't necessarily bad, but Wiess' College Night is better" [@riceinfo-collegenight]. If No-Theme Wiess meant that, it died soon after: from 2003 the books describe a College Night dinner "complete with a theme" and, by 2015, a theme announcement as the highlight [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9] [@oweek-2015 p.24]. But no source says the two are connected, and the name may have meant something else entirely.

## Variants & disputes

- **Dying, then continuing.** The only two sources disagree on its state, two years apart [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@riceinfo-traditions-1999]. Either a 1998–99 event (a themeless party or College Night?) revived it in Wagner's eyes, or the two webmasters simply judged differently.
- **What it was.** The College Night reading above is this site's inference from the same website's words, not a sourced fact. Another possibility is a party or Beer Bike entry that pointedly had no theme. The pages of 1997–2001 contain no other use of "theme".

## Open questions

- What No-Theme Wiess was. Alumni of 1995–2000 would know; the 1997 and 1999 webmasters, David Cunningham and Ray Wagner, are named on their pages [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@riceinfo-traditions-1999].
- Whether the Thresher of 1996–2000 reports a Wiess party or College Night advertised as having no theme [@thresher-portal].
