# TFFW (Team Fun Friday Wiess)

TFFW is Wiess's Friday-afternoon gathering in the Acabowl. The 2016 and 2017 O-Week books describe it: "There's music on the stacks. There's Wiessmen in the acabowl playing volleyball and throwing around a football. Maybe there are puppies? Maybe the A-Team is grilling? or maybe there's a waterslide?", with a theme each time, "a few times a month", run by a TFFW Committee [@oweek-2016 p.31] [@oweek-2017 p.32]. The name is a play on [TFW](team-wiess.md), and its middle letters have never been settled. The college's own documents of 2014–2017 say **Team Fun Friday Wiess**. The Historian gives "Team Family Friday Wiess", "Team Friday Fun Wiess" and "Team Family Friday Fun Wiess", among others, as expansions in use [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b TFFW]. Other colleges hold the same kind of Friday event in their quads: Will Rice's **KITQ** ("Keg in the Quad") and the **FITQ** ("Friday in the Quad") of McMurtry and Duncan [@willrice-traditions] [@oweek-2015 p.52] [@owlmanac-2016 p.13]. The Historian describes TFFW as Wiess's own event in place of those [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b TFFW]. The custom is older than the name. In 1994 the Acabowl was where "TG's are held", and the 60th-anniversary programme of 2017 billed TFFW as "A Traditional Wiess TG" [@handbook-1994] [@wb 20171108201719 http://teamwiess.com/60/]. The name is first attested in June 2014, when the college had two Team Fun Friday Wiess Representatives [@wb 20140627224604 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html].

| FITQ | The Thresher, of Wiess's Friday event, October 2024 | [@thresher-2024-10-mad-announced] [P] |

The abbreviation is stable and the expansion is not. That fits a name built backwards from [TFW](team-wiess.md#the-banners), whose F has been glossed as "Family (or anything else that starts with F)" since 2015 [@oweek-2015 p.119]. "Family" in the later expansions brings the TFW joke into the Friday name.

## Variants & disputes

- **KITQ and FITQ: whose?** The Historian calls them the university's [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b TFFW]. The record shows them as other colleges' events, each in its own quad: KITQ at Will Rice [@willrice-traditions], FITQ at McMurtry in 2015 and Duncan in 2016 [@oweek-2015 p.52] [@owlmanac-2016 p.13]. Read "the university's" as "the rest of campus's": the Friday quad party is a Rice-wide pattern, and TFFW is Wiess's form of it.
- **FITQ at Wiess?** In October 2024 the Thresher reported an announcement "during Wiess' Oct. 18 FITQ" [@thresher-2024-10-mad-announced], although the Historian says Wiess holds TFFWs instead. A reporter may have used the campus's general word for a Wiess TFFW, or the event may have been billed that way that week. One article cannot settle which.
- **Who started it?** A 2017 profile credits a Fellow with starting "Wiess traditions like TFFW" [@oweek-2017 p.51], but Team Fun Friday Wiess Representatives already existed in June 2014 [@wb 20140627224604 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html], and the 60th-anniversary programme calls it "A Traditional Wiess TG" [@wb 20171108201719 http://teamwiess.com/60/]. The profile's "start" is best read loosely, as reviving or reshaping it. The 2014 list is the earliest date for the name, not a founding date.
- **The Associates' post as an ancestor.** The Associates' 2017 "Team Fun Friday" post is not the origin of the name, which is in the college's own records from 2014. It shows that the Associates were invited by 2017 [@wiessassociates-site Team Fun Friday 2017].
- **TG.** In 1994 "TG's" were held in the Acabowl [@handbook-1994]. The 2016 Owlmanac defines a TG as a tailgate, which colleges hold "to celebrate the end of another week" [@owlmanac-2016 p.57]. The 2017 programme calls TFFW a TG [@wb 20171108201719 http://teamwiess.com/60/]. The line from 1994 to 2014 is plausible but not documented, and no source between them mentions Friday TGs at Wiess.

- **How often.** "On one friday afternoon every month" (2019 glossary), "every other week" (2021–25 glossaries), "bi-weekly" (2024–25 committees page) and, in the traditions text unchanged since 2016, "a few times a month" [@oweek-2019 p.15] [@oweek-2021 p.19] [@oweek-2024 p.34] [@oweek-2025 p.39]. The books also settle the FITQ question from the college's side: from 2019 they define TFFW as Wiess's equivalent of Friday in the Quad, and from 2024 add the abbreviation "(FITQ)" [@oweek-2019 p.15] [@oweek-2024 p.25], so the Thresher's "Wiess' Oct. 18 FITQ" was a fair gloss.

## Open questions

- When the name "Team Fun Friday Wiess" was coined. The representatives lists before June 2014 should say. The corpus has none for 2013–14.
- The text of the Associates' "Team Fun Friday Wiess 1/20" post (January 2017). The Associates' site is retired, and the post is known from the archive index only [@wiessassociates-site].
- Whether TFFW has a fixed budget line or representatives today. The books of 2019–2025 name a "TFFW Committee" and, from 2024, put TFFWs under the Socials committee [@oweek-2019 p.32] [@oweek-2024 p.34]. The 2023 list of representatives on the college website does not show TFFW among those captured [@wiess-rice-edu-representatives-2023].
- When "Family" entered the expansion.
- Whether Wiess Friday TGs ran continuously from the 1990s to 2014.
