# Gatsby

Gatsby is the spring-semester counterpart of [DOME](dome.md): a Wiess party at which everyone dresses up [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 Gatsby] [T]. The name suggests the 1920s, but no source says what the dress code is. It is known to this site only from the maintainer's account of current college life (2023–26). No O-Week book, college website or Thresher article in our corpus mentions it, so its age, its origin and how it relates to the college's other spring events are all unknown.

## As the college described it

Nothing in the corpus. The only "Gatsby" in the O-Week books is a 2014 Head Fellow's biography comparing its subject to "the great Gatsby" [@oweek-2014 p.18], which has nothing to do with the party.

## Variants & disputes

- **Counterpart in what sense?** The maintainer pairs Gatsby with DOME [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 Gatsby] [T]: one party a semester, each with a dress code. Whether Gatsby shares any of DOME's other customs is not recorded, and this page does not assume it.
- **Not to be confused with** Wiess's spring public parties—JamFest, then [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md), and since 2025 "Masquerade After Dark" (see [Night of Decadence](night-of-decadence.md)). Nothing in the record links them.

## Open questions

- When Gatsby began and who runs it (a Social VP? a class?). The 2014–15 public budget has no line for it (nor for DOME) among its socials, class and officer lines [@budget-2014-15 p.1]—so either it is younger than 2014, or it was funded outside the college budget, or it ran under another name. Later budgets and Cabinet minutes would settle it.
- The Thresher: a ricethresher.org search for Wiess and "Gatsby" found nothing; the Portal run to 2012 was not searchable from this session.
- Whether Gatsby is Wiess-only or open to other colleges.

- The four O-Week books of 2019–2025 from the maintainer's collection were checked in October 2026: none mentions Gatsby [@oweek-2019 p.31] [@oweek-2021 p.36] [@oweek-2024 p.37] [@oweek-2025 p.38].
