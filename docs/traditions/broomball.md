# Broomball

Hockey, but in sneakers, with brooms. Nobody stays upright for long.

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - Broomball is played on ice in shoes, not skates. Two teams push a ball toward goals with broom-headed sticks [@wikipedia-broomball].
    - Rice students have played it since at least the 1950s [@portal metapth231056 p.3].
    - New Wiessmen played it during O-Week in 2009 [@portal metapth443046 p.11], and by 2012 it was a standard orientation icebreaker [@thresher-2012-08-18-purity-test].
    - Wiess counts it as one of its traditions. The details of how and when Wiess plays are still to be written up.

## The game

- **The ice.** A rink, or any ice you can book [@wikipedia-broomball].
- **The feet.** Shoes, not skates. That's most of the fun [@wikipedia-broomball].
- **The stick.** A broom, or a stick with a broom-shaped head [@wikipedia-broomball].
- **The goal.** Get the ball into the other team's net. Scoring works like hockey [@wikipedia-broomball].

## Broomball at Rice

The Thresher first mentions it in 1957, when two sophomores at the Houston Polar Wave rink "indulged in a little pre-broom ball" [@portal metapth231056 p.3].

Around 1978–80, the Rally Club and two other student societies played broomball at the Sharpstown Ice Rink. In September 1980 they met at Wiess first: "Be at Wiess by 10" [@portal metapth245366 p.5] [@portal metapth245448 p.20].

In the 2000s, broomball turns up all over campus life:

- A student-run athletic club planned broomball tournaments in 2002 [@portal metapth443025 p.6].
- "Rice Night" at Houston Aeros hockey games ended with a college-vs-college broomball tournament on the ice in 2006, and with broomball games for students in 2009 and 2010 [@portal metapth443213 p.19] [@portal metapth443061 p.23] [@portal metapth398458 p.19].
- In 2006, a columnist bragged that the north colleges (Wiess among them by then) beat the south colleges at "Capture the cone, broomball, flag football, you name it" [@portal metapth443027 p.3].

## Broomball at Wiess

The clearest Wiess moment so far is O-Week 2009. A Thresher photo caption: "Rice may not have a hockey team, but that didn't discourage new Wiessmen from displaying their talents on the broomball ice. Next stop: Houston Aeros?" [@portal metapth443046 p.11]

How Wiess plays now, and when, is for Wiessmen to tell. If you've played, [add your story](../about/contribute.md).

See also [Wiess Skate](wiess-skate.md), the college's other night on the ice.
