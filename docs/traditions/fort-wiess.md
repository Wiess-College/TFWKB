# Fort Wiess

Fort Wiess was a wooden structure Wiess built for the Beer Bike parade in the college's last two springs at Old Wiess, 2001 and 2002. The Thresher's Beer Bike photographs of 5 April 2002 show "Team Wiess's battle sow flying high in the sky"—the inflatable pig—over "the Wiess fort" [@thresher-2002-04-05 p.6], and the Beer Bike coverage of 6 April 2001 belongs to the same parade season [@thresher-2001-04-06 p.29]. That is the whole of the record. The college's own websites of 2001–02 do not mention it, no O-Week book remembers it, and it never entered a glossary. It matters mainly as a correction: Wiess's parade build in those years was a fort, not a pig, and the first wooden War Pig came a decade later, in 2012 [@campanile-2012 p.172].

## As the college described it

The college did not describe it. No page of the riceinfo or teamwiess.com sites captured between 2000 and 2003 mentions a fort, and the 2003 O-Week book's history, written a year later, goes from the 1988 jack to the new building without it [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.3]. The only words are the Thresher's captions [@thresher-2002-04-05 p.6].

## Variants & disputes

- **A wooden pig in 2002?** The record for Beer Bike 2002 has an inflatable pig in the air and a wooden fort on the ground [@thresher-2002-04-05 p.6]; the first wooden pig is 2012 [@campanile-2012 p.172]. Anyone who remembers a wooden pig from the Old Wiess years is probably remembering the fort with the balloon above it. See [The War Pig](warpig.md) for the pigs of 2000–2004.
- **Which pig.** The Thresher calls the 2002 balloon a "battle sow"; the college's glossary called it the War Pig [@oweek-2003 p.4]. Both names were current—see [Battle Sows & Powderpuff](battle-sows-powderpuff.md).
- **One season or two.** The historian's notes date the fort "2001–02"; only the 2002 captions name it. Whether the 2001 parade also had it is not confirmed from the pages cited here.

## Open questions

- What the fort looked like, who built it, and whether it had a name beyond "the Wiess fort". The Thresher pages of 6 April 2001 and 5 April 2002 should be read in full and transcribed; neither is in the corpus, and the Portal was not reachable from the build environment.
- Whether the Campanile of 2001 or 2002 photographed it. The college's Campanile scans stop at 1999 and resume at 2012 [@campanile-1999 p.238] [@campanile-2012 p.172].
- Whether Wiess built a parade structure other than a pig in any other year. Nothing in the corpus says so; the parade's history is on the [Beer Bike](beer-bike.md) page.
