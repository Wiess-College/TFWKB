# Pumpkin Grades

Your first report card at Rice shows up around Halloween. Hence the pumpkin.

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - "Pumpkin Grades" are the mid-semester grades Rice gives first-years in their first fall [@oweek-2003 p.6] [@rice-registrar-midterm-grades].
    - They're just for your information. They never go on your transcript [@rice-registrar-midterm-grades].
    - They come out "around Halloween," say the O-Week books since 2015 [@oweek-2015 p.122].

## What they are

Every O-Week glossary since 2003 defines them. The 2015 version is still the one in use: "Mid-semester grades given to new students in the fall to indicate how they are doing in their classes. They come out around Halloween." [@oweek-2015 p.122] [@oweek-2025 p.28]

The Registrar calls them mid-term grades. They only apply to first-years and first-year transfers, and they're "purely informational" [@rice-registrar-midterm-grades].

## Why "pumpkin"?

We haven't found an explanation in writing yet. Halloween timing is the obvious answer, but the sources we have don't say so. The Registrar's page doesn't use the word [@rice-registrar-midterm-grades].

It's Rice slang, not just Wiess slang. The campus-wide Owlmanac defined it too in 2016 [@owlmanac-2016 p.56].

??? info "Arguments & loose ends"
    **Where sources disagree.**

    - *When exactly?* "Around Halloween" [@oweek-2015 p.122]. No source gives a fixed date; it follows the academic calendar.
    - *Pumpkin Caroling?* [Pumpkin Caroling](pumpkin-caroling.md) shares the season and the pumpkin, but no source connects the two.

    **Still don't know.**

    - When the slang started. The 1994 Freshman Handbook glossary doesn't have it [@handbook-1994]; the 2003 book does [@oweek-2003 p.6]. The 1994–2003 Thresher might pin it down.
    - Whether Wiess does anything social around Pumpkin Grades. Nothing in writing so far.
