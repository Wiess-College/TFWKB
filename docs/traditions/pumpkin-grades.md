# Pumpkin Grades

"Pumpkin Grades" is Rice slang for the mid-semester grades the university gives first-year students in their first fall: informational only, never on the transcript [@rice-registrar-midterm-grades], and arriving, as the O-Week books put it from 2015, "around Halloween" [@oweek-2015 p.122]. Every Wiess O-Week glossary from 2003 to 2017 defines the term, and so did the university-wide Owlmanac in 2016 [@oweek-2003 p.6] [@owlmanac-2016 p.56].

## As the college described it

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "Pumpkin grades—1. Mid-semester grades given to freshmen in the fall." [@oweek-2003 p.6]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2015"
    "Pumpkin Grades—Mid-semester grades given to new students in the fall to indicate how they are doing in their classes. They come out around Halloween." [@oweek-2015 p.122]

The books only ever define the academic term, and still did in 2025 [@oweek-2025 p.28].

## Variants & disputes

- **When.** "Around Halloween" in the books from 2015 [@oweek-2015 p.122]; no source gives a fixed date, which follows the academic calendar each year.
- **Why "pumpkin"?** No source explains the name. The Halloween timing is the obvious explanation; the university itself does not use the word [@rice-registrar-midterm-grades].
- **Pumpkin Caroling.** [Pumpkin Caroling](pumpkin-caroling.md) shares the season and the pumpkin, and in the glossaries the two entries sit near each other, but no source connects them.

## Open questions

- When "Pumpkin Grades" entered Rice slang. The 1994 Freshman Handbook glossary does not have it [@handbook-1994]; the 2003 book does [@oweek-2003 p.6]. The Thresher (Portal, 1994–2003) would date it more closely; the Portal was not searchable from this session.
- Whether Wiess marks Pumpkin Grades socially: the written record says nothing so far.
