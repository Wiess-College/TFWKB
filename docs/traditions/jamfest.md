# JamFest

JamFest was Wiess's spring concert in the Acabowl: an afternoon-to-night bill of Rice, Houston and, later, touring bands, with T-shirts, hamburgers and towels on the grass. It is first described on the 1997 website as "an annual party/concert held at Wiess every spring" with a list of alumni bands [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html], was "one of the coolest parties at Rice… Held during Owl Weekend" in the 2003 book [@oweek-2003 p.4], and by 2005 ran ten hours with a headliner brought in from Illinois [@teamwiess-jamfest-site]. The glossary merged it with the new spring party as "Jamfest/Wiess Day" in 2010 and 2011 [@oweek-2010 p.91], and the 2014 book wrote its obituary: "things like Jamfest have come and gone" [@oweek-2014 p.37]. Its successor is [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md).

## As the college described it

!!! quote "riceinfo site, 'Acabowl Muzak', 1997"
    "For those who don't know, JamFest is an annual party/concert held at Wiess every spring, featuring lots of Wiess/Rice/Houston bands… Unfortunately, due to a sudden implosion of the Rice music scene, neither the Hartvigs, Dyn@mutt or Sailplane exist anymore, so they couldn't play." [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "NOD isn't the only Wiess party that's growing and evolving. If you were anywhere near Wiess during Owl Weekend, you should know exactly what we're talking about: Jamfest. What started as a handful of makeshift bands playing in the Acabowl has become an all-day festival featuring some of the best bands from Rice and beyond… Spend the whole day lying on a towel, eating hamburgers and hanging with your friends… Ask any other college. It's like Hammer used to say, 'Can't touch this.'" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9]

!!! quote "teamwiess.com/Jamfest, 2005"
    "It's big, loud, and the most rockin' party at Rice. Jamfest features Rice University's top bands as well as groups from all over Houston, TX. This year, Victory Records' The Junior Varsity will be making the trip down from Illinois to rock it in the Wiess Acabowl." [@wb 20070206202205 http://www.teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2010"
    "Since moving into our new digs we've maintained what makes Wiess what it is and have added some new things: namely Wiess Day in the spring to celebrate all things Wiess, a cornhole tournament during the Beer Bike barbecue and a more modern version of Wiess's beloved Jamfest." [@oweek-2010 p.39]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2014"
    "Wiess Day has become an annual tradition, as has the O-Week mango-eating contest, and things like Jamfest have come and gone." [@oweek-2014 p.37]

## Variants & disputes

- **Did Wiess Day replace JamFest, or rename it?** The 2008 book has both as separate events — Wiess Day a new party thrown by the Socials, Jamfest the concert [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8] [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.9] — and the 2010 and 2011 books still describe both while the glossary runs them together as "Jamfest/Wiess Day" with the Jamfest definition [@oweek-2010 p.91]. The traditions index on this site reads the 2015 website's "a new tradition began" as a rebranding remembered as a founding; the 2008 book shows instead a new event that absorbed the old one within a few years. See [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md).
- **When in spring.** "Owl Weekend" (the admitted-students weekend) in 2003–2007 [@oweek-2003 p.4]; "the spring" from 2008 [@oweek-2008 part 7 p.4]. The 2005 and 2007 shows were both in mid-April [@teamwiess-jamfest-site].
- **A wrong weekday.** The 2007 site says "Friday April 14th, 2007"; 14 April 2007 was a Saturday and the Friday was the 13th [@wb 20070709183135 http://teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm]. The 2005 date ("Friday April 15th, 2005") is a real Friday.
- **Bowling for Soup.** "The last two years" in 2003, i.e. 2002 and 2003 [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9]; "before" in 2006–2010 [@oweek-2006 p.44] [@oweek-2010 p.45]. No Thresher confirmation in the corpus.
- **What the 1997 page dates.** The Cunningham text ("Last Updated 8/11/97") says Jamfest "is coming up quickly", so it was written before a spring show — 1997 or 1998 — and frozen when the page was rebuilt in August 1997 [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html].

## Open questions

- When JamFest began. "Annual" in 1997 and "alums" from a defunct Rice scene imply several years by then; no source gives a first year.
- The 2002 and 2003 line-ups, and whether the Thresher covered Bowling for Soup at Wiess.
- The last JamFest: 2011 is the last book to describe one [@oweek-2011 p.46]; the 2012 and 2013 books are not in the corpus.
- Photographs: the 1997 page had a `jamfest1` image and the 2005–07 sub-site a poster and photos, all in the mirror but unexamined [@teamwiess-jamfest-site].
