# JamFest

Bands in the Acabowl, towels on the grass, hamburgers all day. JamFest was Wiess's spring concert until Wiess Day took its place.

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - JamFest was Wiess's spring concert in the Acabowl, with Rice, Houston and later touring bands [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html] [@teamwiess-jamfest-site].
    - It started at least as far back as March 1987, as "an open musical talent show" in the Commons [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest].
    - By 2005 it ran ten hours, with a headliner from Illinois [@teamwiess-jamfest-site] [@oweek-2006 p.23].
    - In 2014 the book wrote its obituary: "things like Jamfest have come and gone" [@oweek-2014 p.37]. Its successor is [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md).

## From talent show to festival

In March 1987 the Thresher announced "JamFest, an open musical talent show, in the Commons" [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest]. By 1997 it was "an annual party/concert held at Wiess every spring, featuring lots of Wiess/Rice/Houston bands" [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html].

The 2003 book tells it this way: "What started as a handful of makeshift bands playing in the Acabowl has become an all-day festival" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9]. In 2002 and 2003 the headliner was the Grammy-nominated Bowling for Soup [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9].

## The big years

JamFest 2005 ran from 4 p.m. to 2 a.m., with fourteen acts on two stages, plus face painting and henna [@wb 20070206202205 http://www.teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm] [@wb 20070206202123 http://www.teamwiess.com/Jamfest/playlist.htm]. The 2007 show started at 2 p.m. [@wb 20070709183135 http://teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm]. It used to be held on Owl Weekend, when admitted students visit [@oweek-2003 p.4].

## Fade out

Wiess Day began in spring 2008 as a separate party [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8]. By 2010 the glossary lumped them together as "Jamfest/Wiess Day" [@oweek-2010 p.91]. 2011 is the last book that describes a JamFest [@oweek-2011 p.46].

??? quote "In the college's own words"
    "Unfortunately, due to a sudden implosion of the Rice music scene, neither the Hartvigs, Dyn@mutt or Sailplane exist anymore, so they couldn't play." [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html]

    "Spend the whole day lying on a towel, eating hamburgers and hanging with your friends… It's like Hammer used to say, 'Can't touch this.'" [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9]

    "It's big, loud, and the most rockin' party at Rice." [@wb 20070206202205 http://www.teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm]

    "…a more modern version of Wiess's beloved Jamfest." [@oweek-2010 p.39]

??? info "Arguments & loose ends"
    **Where sources disagree.**

    - **Did Wiess Day replace JamFest, or rename it?** In 2008 they were separate events [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.8] [@oweek-2008 part 3 p.9]. In 2010–11 the books still describe both, while the glossary merges them [@oweek-2010 p.91]. So a new event absorbed the old one over a few years. See [Wiess Day](wiess-day.md).
    - **When in spring?** Owl Weekend in 2003–2007 [@oweek-2003 p.4]; just "the spring" from 2008 [@oweek-2008 part 7 p.4]. In 1987 it was 27 March [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest].
    - **Commons or Acabowl?** The 1987 notice puts it in the Commons [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest]; every later source puts it in the Acabowl.
    - **A wrong weekday.** The 2007 site says "Friday April 14th, 2007," but that was a Saturday [@wb 20070709183135 http://teamwiess.com/Jamfest/main.htm].
    - **Bowling for Soup.** 2002 and 2003 per the 2003 book; "before" in later books [@oweek-2003 35-44 wiess.pdf p.9] [@oweek-2006 p.44] [@oweek-2010 p.45]. No Thresher confirmation yet.
    - **The 1997 page** was written just before a spring show, 1997 or 1998 [@wb 19990220115332 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/traditions/jamfest.html].

    **Still don't know.**

    - Did it begin in 1987, or earlier? The 1987 notice is the earliest found [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest].
    - The 2002 and 2003 line-ups.
    - The last JamFest. The 2012 and 2013 books are missing [@oweek-2011 p.46].
    - Photos from 1997 and 2005–07 are in the mirror but unexamined [@teamwiess-jamfest-site].
