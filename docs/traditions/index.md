# Traditions

This page lists every Wiess tradition we've found, A to Z, with a line on what each one is and whether it's still going.

"Traditions come and go, like anything else… but don't worry; somebody'll come up with something else cool to do. Change can be good." That's the 1997 Wiess website [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]. Still true.

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - Everything Wiess does, A to Z, with one line each and whether it's still going.
    - **Alive** = still happening. **Changed** = still around, but different. **Retired** = a source says it ended. **Last seen <year>** = the latest mention we've found so far (which doesn't mean it stopped).
    - Want the oldest first, with dates? Open the receipts at the bottom.

??? info "The receipts: Wiess ranks its own traditions, 1997 and 1999"
    In August 1997 David Cunningham's traditions page on the first Wiess website sorted them into three tiers [@riceinfo-traditions-1997].

    - **Traditions Older Than Time** ("These traditions need never die!"): TEAM WIESS, the Ubangee, "the mighty War Pig", Beer Bike, Pumpkin Caroling, the Freshman Jack and College Night.
    - **Traditions We'd Sure Like To See Continue**: Wiess Films, the Battle Sows, four-square in the Acabowl, the Freshman Weenie Roast, Unnecessary Tie Day, "What time is it?", Big Bang, the WiessCrack, JamFest, FilmFest, Norse Night and "the utter and complete obliteration of 13".
    - **Traditions That Sadly Seem To Be Dying Out**: Talleyboo, *The Princess Bride* and No-Theme Wiess.

    Ray Wagner's December 1999 revision moved Unnecessary Tie Day and Norse Night down to "Dying Out" and promoted No-Theme Wiess out of it [@riceinfo-traditions-1999]. Four-square stayed in the middle both years.
