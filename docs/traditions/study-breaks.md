# Study breaks

A study break is a short, informal event, usually in the evening and usually with food, that someone at the college throws so that people can stop working for half an hour. Nearly everyone at Wiess holds them. The O-Week books and websites since 2003 credit study breaks to the Masters and Magisters, the Resident Associates, the Associates, the First-Year Representatives, the Outreach Committee, the Wiess Mentors, appointed Study Break Reps (2005–2012) and Study Break Coordinators (2014–15), and other representatives with ideas of their own, from a Canadian-themed study break to dog study breaks [@oweek-2003 intro p.7] [@wb 20070709183121 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?module=page&page=representatives] [@wb 20140711230215 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html] [@wb 20170421224822 http://teamwiess.com/reps.html]. Rice's own Rice Program Council holds "study breaks during finals" for the whole campus, the largest being the President's (and Dean's) Study Break [@oweek-2014 p.106] [@owlmanac-2016 p.39]. The maintainer notes that groups hold them, and would like a glossary of them some day [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05c study breaks] [T]. This page is the start of one: what the record names, by whom and when. It is far from complete, since most study breaks are announced on a listserv or a chalkboard and never written down anywhere that lasts.

!!! note "Add a study break"
    The table below is built only from what the O-Week books, the college websites, the governing documents and the budget mention. If you held, went to or remember a Wiess study break (who threw it, what it was, roughly when), put it in the **Commons** with your name and class year, and an editor will add it here as testimony `[T]`. Pancakes, barbacoa, liquid nitrogen, cupcakes, a zoo trip or records: if it had a name, it belongs in this list.

## Screen printing

Screen printing T-shirts, once an O-Week activity, is now held as a study break [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05c screen printing] [T]. The written record has the screen-printing representative ("YEAH WIESS", 2010–2017), whose 2017 holders set out to "Make screen-printing T-shirts more accessible for Wiessmen in Wiess-related activities" and to hold a "T-shirt design contest (one each semester)" [@wb 20170421224822 http://teamwiess.com/reps.html] [P], but no study break by that name; as a study break it is testimony only. See [Beer Bike](beer-bike.md#screen-printing-yeah-wiess-yeah-beer-yeah-water).

## As the college described it

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2003"
    "The masters throw study breaks, are available if you are sick or need someone to talk to, offer academic advising whenever you need it, and are great to talk to." [@oweek-2003 intro p.7]

!!! quote "teamwiess.com, Cabinet minutes, September 2009"
    "As those of us who were here last year know, the Masters hosted a Middle Eastern study break, complete with roast lamb, and it was excellent." [@wb 20090917020007 http://teamwiess.com/cabinetminutes.php]

!!! quote "O-Week Book 2024"
    "The events and study breaks we host tend to be informal and fairly unstructured. We try to host something for students about twice a month like s'mores night, a movie watch party, a homemade cookie baking session, a zoo field trip, or invite you just to listen to records." [@oweek-2024 p.30]

## Variants & disputes

- **A rep, or everyone's job?** From at least 2005–06 to 2015 the college had appointed Study Break Reps or Coordinators [@wb 20070709183121 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?module=page&page=representatives] [@wb 20150313224645 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html]. The position is absent from the 2015–2017 lists and from every list since 2020 [@wb 20150612230047 http://teamwiess.com/reps.html] [@wiess-rice-edu-representatives-2023]; by then the study break had become something every group does. See [Representatives](../governance/representatives.md).
- **The word.** The books use "study break" both for the event and for any rest from work ("Filmfest can be the perfect way to take a study break") [@oweek-2015 p.23]. The table keeps only the uses that name an event or a host, plus a few of the general ones for color.
