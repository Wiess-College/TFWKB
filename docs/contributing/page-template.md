## Notes on the sections

**Summary first.** Someone who reads only the first paragraph should leave with the right picture. Do not open with "The history of X is long and storied."

**Timeline rows are claims, not prose.** If a row needs two citations, give it two. If you cannot cite a row, it belongs in Open questions ("Said to have begun c.1992; no source yet") or in the Commons.

**"As the college described it" is quotation, not paraphrase.** Short, dated, cited, in the college's own words. It is where the humour lives, and it is where drift shows: the War Pig was "the Wiess mascot" in 1994 and 2003, "Former Wiess mascot" from 2006 to 2011, and "embodied by the giant wooden pig built by the Class of 2012" from 2014.

**Variants & disputes is mandatory when it applies.** Silence on a known disagreement is the one thing this site must never do.

**Retired traditions** get the same template plus a line in the summary on when and why they stopped, as best the record shows.
