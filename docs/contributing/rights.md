# Rights

**Text on this site** is © its contributors and licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): quote it, reuse it, build on it, with credit and under the same terms. **The tools** in `tools/` and `hooks/` are MIT. The point of both is that the work can be reconstituted anywhere if this site goes away.

This site holds condensed knowledge and cites the evidence; it does not re-host it.

| Source | Treatment |
|---|---|
| The college's own documents—O-Week books, constitutions, bylaws, rules, its websites | quoted freely; they are Wiess's own words |
| The Rice Thresher | brief quotation with citation; pages are linked at the Portal to Texas History or ricethresher.org, not copied |
| The Campanile | brief quotation and description; scans stay in the college's own `artifacts/` collection and are not published here |
| Rice History Corner (Melissa Kean) and its commenters | brief quotation with attribution and date; linked, not copied. Commenters are named by the public handle they chose |
| Rice Magazine, other publications | brief quotation, linked |
| Third-party photographs (Delany 2002, the architects, Flickr) | described and linked; not reproduced |
| Photographs and documents given to the college (the pitch deck, the pig photographs) | described; published only with the owner's permission, which is recorded in the artifact's README |

**People:** Students appear here as public sources name them in their college roles—authors of O-Week book pieces, elected officers, people quoted in the Thresher, commenters on a public blog. No rosters are kept. No name is paired with a room number, phone number or address, even where a 1990s source printed them. Anyone named may ask to be referred to by role instead; open an issue.

**Takedown:** If something here is yours and should not be, open an issue; it will be removed first and discussed after.
## Photographs

Photographs make the pages worth reading, so the site shows them as web-size copies. A photograph may go on the site only if one of these is true:

- **You own it**: you took it, or the copyright is otherwise yours.
- **You have permission**: the copyright holder agreed, in writing (an email is enough), to its being published here. Send the permission to the Historian, who keeps it.
- **Its license allows it**: it is in the public domain, or released under CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA (any version), or a license at least as open.

Not enough on their own: finding it online, "all rights reserved", or a license with NC (non-commercial) or ND (no derivatives). Anyone may copy this site, and the photos are meant to travel with it to the Internet Archive, so a photo's terms must allow copying with credit and resizing.

Say which applies in the photo's `credit` in `sources/photo-placements.yaml`: who made it, then "used with permission" or the license ("CC BY 4.0"). A photo that meets none of these is described and linked, not shown.
