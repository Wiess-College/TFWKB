# Wiess House / Wilson House

The Magisters' house[^magister] has had two names and at least two buildings. At Old Wiess it was **Wiess House**, "a small house attached to the college", where the Hutchinsons hosted the Sunday *Simpsons* gathering in the 1990s [@riceinfo-masters]; it had been built with the commons when the dormitory became a college in 1957 [@edesigns-old-wiess-2002]. At New Wiess the Magisters' house is **Wilson House**, "Dedicated in 2011 as a memorial to beloved RA William 'Dr. Bill' Wilson… the new home of the Wiess Masters, and the only building anywhere at Rice named after a former master or RA" [@wiess-60th-faq-2017 p.4]. Every O-Week glossary since 2003 has had an entry for it under one name or another, and since 2006 the same promise: "Where everybody knows your name, and the door is always open" [@oweek-2006 p.84]. The house has had its own Twitter account, @ricewilsonhouse, since the Byrds' time [@wb 20140627224545 http://teamwiess.com/master.html]. For the people who lived in it, see [Magisters](../people/masters-and-magisters.md) and the [Core Team](../people/core-team.md); for the man it is named for, [Resident Associates](../people/resident-associates.md).

## Variants & disputes

- **Which building is which.** The record shows a house "attached to the college" in 1994 [@riceinfo-masters]; a house "on the Inner Loop", regretted as distant, in 2003 [@oweek-2003 p.4]; a house "right next door to Wiess" in 2006 [@oweek-2006 p.84]; and in 2011 a "new home of the Wiess Masters" dedicated as Wilson House [@wiess-60th-faq-2017 p.4]. The simplest reading is that the old Wiess House on the Inner Loop outlived Old Wiess and served Dan Kalb and Katharine Donato, that a house adjacent to the new building was in use by 2006, and that it (or a successor) was dedicated and named in 2011. No source in the corpus says whether the 2006 house and the 2011 Wilson House are the same structure. The master plan's "individual masters house" [@machado-silvetti-wiess] suggests a purpose-built house was always intended.
- **"Dedicated in 2011 as a memorial."** The 2017 FAQ is a retrospective written by the 60th Anniversary Committee [@wiess-60th-faq-2017 p.4]. The contemporary trace is the 2011 glossary's second sense, "Dr. Bill House" [@oweek-2011 p.93], and the "Wilson House" name in every book from 2014. Dr. Bill had left the RA post after 2005–06 [@wb 20070709183122 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?module=page&page=resident_associates] and is "Ex-Wiess RA" by the 2010 glossary [@oweek-2010 p.90].
- **Master's, Masters', Magister's.** "Wiess Master House" (2006–2015), "Wilson House (Wiess Master House)" (2014, 2016), "Wiess Master House (Wilson House)" (2015), "Wilson House (Wiess Magister's House)" (2017) [@oweek-2006 p.84] [@oweek-2015 p.119] [@oweek-2017 p.15]. The 2017 change follows the college's switch to "Magisters" that year — see [Magisters](../people/masters-and-magisters.md).
- **The 2008 entry is clipped** in the extracted text ("Mike and Denise's house. Where everybody knows your name, and") [@oweek-2008 part 7 p.4]; the printed page presumably completes the sentence as in 2007 and 2010.

## Open questions

- Was the Wilson House of 2011 newly built, or the existing adjacent house renamed? Rice News or the Thresher for 2011 should carry the dedication.
- Dr. Bill Wilson's dates. The house is called a memorial in 2017 and Kean writes of him in the past tense by January 2014 [@rhc 2014-01-24 friday-afternoon-follies-dr-bill-in-a-skirt], but no source in the corpus gives a date of death.
- Where exactly the old Wiess House stood ("on the north side of the college" per one alumnus [@rhc 2011-05-06 friday-afternoon-follies-plus-a-campus-quiz comment by Richard Miller, 10 May 2011]) and when it came down.
- The @ricewilsonhouse Twitter account has two Wayback captures (2016, 2020) that are not yet in the corpus; they would show who ran it and when it started.
- "A Celebration of the Byrds 4/25" (2016) is known from the Associates' site index [@wiessassociates-site]; the post itself has not yet been read.


[^magister]: Called "Master" until 2017; this site uses "Magister" for every period. Quotations keep their original wording.
