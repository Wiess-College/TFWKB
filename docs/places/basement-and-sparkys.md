# The basement and Sparky's

"Sparky's" is the college's game and party room, and it has been in two places. In Old Wiess the basement under the central wing held the washers and dryers and the game room. By 1994 the game room was called Sparky's, after "Sparky", a famously indecent NOD decoration of c.1980 (see [Night of Decadence](../traditions/night-of-decadence.md)). It had the big-screen TV and VCR, and FilmFest was held there [@handbook-1994]. By 1999 the college's own website said the basement was known as Sparky's only "at least in the distant past" [@wb 19990219085339 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/people/cabinet.html]. New Wiess's basement is a storage space: "under the servery" in 2003–11, "under the Commons" from 2014, later used for screen-printing shirts [@oweek-2003 p.3] [@oweek-2014 p.102]. The name came back in 2010 for "the newly renovated fourth floor hangout space" [@oweek-2010 p.91]. The O-Week books say that room "used to be a Wiess storage closet" [@oweek-2014 p.46]. It is very probably the storage "War room" that Cabinet voted to empty and refit in August 2009 [@wb 20090903015953 http://teamwiess.com/cabinetminutes.php]. The maintainer confirms that Sparky's is the fourth-floor party room today [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 Sparky's]. The building's other shared rooms are on [Rooms and spaces of New Wiess](rooms-and-spaces.md). Next door is the Movie Room, called the "TV room" in 2003–11, which took over FilmFest from the old basement [@oweek-2003 p.4] [@oweek-2014 p.103].

## The darkroom and the screens

The New Wiess basement, under the Commons, holds what is left of two of the college's crafts. The maintainer: the old T-shirt screens are still there, in the area of the former photo-development darkroom [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05c screen printing] [T].

**The darkroom.** The Bylaws listed "dark room equipment" among the college property held by committee chairmen from at least 1999 to 2011 [@wb 19990220014631 http://riceinfo.rice.edu:80/projects/colleges/wiess/rules/bylaws.html] [@bylaws-2010 Art. III §2] [P]; the RA of the time, Dr. Bill Wilson, was known for "taking pictures of almost every Wiess event" [@riceinfo-associates]. By the Bylaws of 2020 the darkroom equipment is gone from the list and "shirt-screening equipment" is in it [@bylaws-2020 Art. III §2] [P]. No source says where the darkroom was in either building, or when it closed; its location in the New Wiess basement is testimony only.

**The screens.** The glossaries of 2014 to 2019 define the basement as "The area under the Commons used for storage and shirt screen making" (2014), then "storage and shirt screening" [@oweek-2014 p.102] [@oweek-2015 p.118] [@oweek-2019 p.14] [P]. The printing, the "YEAH WIESS" representative who ran it (2010–2017), and the Beer Bike shirts are on [Beer Bike](../traditions/beer-bike.md#screen-printing-yeah-wiess-yeah-beer-yeah-water).

## Variants & disputes

- **Where the name went between 1999 and 2010.** In 1999 Sparky's was the basement name "at least in the distant past" [@wb 19990219085339 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/people/cabinet.html]. No source between 2001 and 2009 uses it, and the 2003–09 New Wiess sources speak of a "TV/Rec Room" [@oweek-2006 p.15], a "Game Room" [@wb 20080709214915 http://teamwiess.com/index.php?r=otherrooms] and a "War room" [@wb 20090903015953 http://teamwiess.com/cabinetminutes.php]. The name reappears in 2010 [@oweek-2010 p.91]. Whoever named the 2010 room either remembered Old Wiess or revived the name deliberately. The [NOD page](../traditions/night-of-decadence.md) leaves the same question open.
- **Was the "War room" Sparky's?** The evidence points that way, but no source says so. The War room was a storage room with a pool table and dead Skee-Ball in August 2009, with a plan for poker tables and track lighting [@wb 20090903015953 http://teamwiess.com/cabinetminutes.php]. In 2010 Sparky's is "newly renovated", with "mood lighting" [@oweek-2010 p.91]; by 2014 it has "poker setups" and "used to be a Wiess storage closet… transformed in the past five years" [@oweek-2014 p.103] [@oweek-2014 p.46]. The 2009 Game Room "currently being used for storage" with a pool table may be the same room under a third name [@wb 20090827015957 http://teamwiess.com/rooms.php]. None of the three sources gives a floor for it.
- **Movie Room/Sparky's.** The 2010 map gives one fourth-floor label, "4: Movie Room/Sparky's" [@oweek-2010 p.14]; from 2015 the map shows two rooms [@oweek-2015 p.19]. This may be only a change in labelling. The 2014 text already has them as neighbours [@oweek-2014 p.46].
- **Under the servery or under the Commons?** New Wiess's basement is "under the servery" 2003–2011 and "under the Commons" 2014–2017 [@oweek-2011 p.92] [@oweek-2014 p.102]. The servery and the Commons are one complex [@rice-facilities-first-100-years], so this may be the same space described from a different side. The 2014 text also makes it easier to reach ("Can be accessed using your room key"), against "hard-to-get to" before.
- **The game room's furniture.** 1994 lists a wide-screen TV and VCR [@handbook-1994]; 1999 a pool table, ping-pong table and big-screen TV [@wb 19990219085339 http://riceinfo.rice.edu/projects/colleges/wiess/people/cabinet.html]. The pool table that crossed to New Wiess was to go to the Upper Commons in 2009 [@wb 20090903015953 http://teamwiess.com/cabinetminutes.php], and the 2014 glossary has one there [@oweek-2014 p.103]. Whether it is the Old Wiess table is unknown.

## Open questions

- Where the darkroom was, in Old Wiess and in New Wiess, and when it stopped being used for film; whether any of its equipment survives beside the screens.
- An inventory of the screens in the basement: which designs, and which years.
- Was the 2009 "War room" refit approved at Summit 2009, and what did it cost? Fall 2009 Cabinet minutes or the 2009–10 budget would settle whether it became Sparky's.
- Why "War room"? Possibly after the [War Pig](../traditions/warpig.md), but no source says so.
- The maintainer lists "the basement (current and old)" as a gap; what is the basement used for in 2026? [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 the basement]
- The Old Wiess basement "library" of 1957 [@wb 20040416083807 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/35-44%20wiess.pdf p.2]: did the game room replace it?
- When the Old Wiess game room was "repainted and rededcorated in black and gold" ("recently" in 1994) [@handbook-1994].
- Photographs of either Sparky's. None are in the corpus, and none of the captions on Delany's 2002 photographs of Old Wiess mentions the basement [@delany-about-wiess-2002].

- "Slowly transformed in the past five years" is the 2014 sentence, reprinted unchanged through 2025 [@oweek-2014 p.46] [@oweek-2025 p.11]; it dates the transformation to c.2009–14, not to the year of each book.
