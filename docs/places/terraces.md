# The terraces and the Bacabowl

Wiess's "Aca-" place names move around, and the books and the people who live in the building do not agree on them. In Old Wiess the **Backabowl** (also spelled Bacabowl) was the second, quieter courtyard behind the three-storey central wing, "Where most of the amateur and shy sun-bathers can be found" [@handbook-1994] [@wb 20020615221715 http://riceinfo.rice.edu:80/projects/colleges/wiess/college/photos/backabowl.html]. New Wiess has one courtyard, and the back-of-the-house names passed to its terraces. Machado and Silvetti's building has "a servery crowned by a large public terrace overlooking the playing fields", shared by the Wiess and Hanszen dining halls [@machado-silvetti-wiess] [@rice-facilities-first-100-years]. In print that roof terrace was the **Backaterrace** in 2003, "the terrace behind the Commons that we unfortunately have to share with Hanszen" [@oweek-2003 p.3], and the **Acaterrace** from 2006 to 2017 [@oweek-2006 p.83] [@oweek-2017 p.14]; in 2014 the books added a **Bacaterrace**, "The Fourth Floor balcony. Great spot to hang out or watch the sunset" [@oweek-2014 p.102]. Present usage, as the maintainer, a resident since 2023, gives it in October 2026, is different [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b terraces]: the **Acaterrace** is a small terrace between UpCo (the Upper Commons) and the second-floor rooms, a small outdoor space above the firepit; "Bacaterrace" for the servery roof is "just what Hanszen calls it"; and the fourth-floor balcony is **Toke**, a word that can also mean the walkway along the fourth floor—"Toke is canon". For present-day names this page follows the maintainer; the books' usage is kept below as the history of the names, and the difference is set out under Variants & disputes. The utility stack on the servery roof has a horned owl laid into its brick [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 owl]; see [Building lore](lore.md).

How to read it. In print, one space, the terrace on the servery roof, was Backaterrace (2003), then Acaterrace (2006–17), then **Bacaterrace** (2019–21); a second, the fourth-floor balcony, was Bacaterrace (2014–17), then Acaterrace (2019), then plain "Fourth Floor Balcony" (2021), and Toke in a 2025 profile; and in 2021 a third, the small terrace "connecting the second floor and the Upper Commons", became the Acaterrace [@oweek-2019 p.14] [@oweek-2021 p.18] [@oweek-2025 p.48]. The 2021 book is the first written source to match present usage. In 2026 speech the three names sit differently: the Acaterrace is a small terrace by UpCo, the fourth-floor balcony is Toke, and the servery roof has no Wiess name the maintainer vouches for—"Bacaterrace" is Hanszen's (and his own first notes of the same day called it "aka the Backaterrace") [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 terraces] [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b terraces]. In print, "Backabowl" and "Bacabowl" refer only to Old Wiess. The 2026 Bacabowl is a New Wiess place no written source describes.

## Toke

**What.** The fourth-floor balcony of New Wiess; the word can also mean the walkway along the fourth floor. "Toke is canon" [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b Toke]. It is a place name, used as one; the record does not explain where the word came from.

**First attested.** In print in an O-Week book, 2025: a Fellow "hanging out at Toke for the views (sort of)" [@oweek-2025 p.48]. In writing, July 2022: "TOKE" painted on a graffiti-covered table in an O-Week photograph on the college website (below) [@wb 20220709040142 http://teamwiess.com/images/oweek2022/wiessicles.jpg]. The photograph does not show where the table stands. As a named place, 5 October 2026 [T]; the maintainer's first notes of the same day listed it without saying what it was [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 places].

**Before the name.** The space is the one the 2014–2017 glossaries call the **Bacaterrace**, "The Fourth Floor balcony. Great spot to hang out or watch the sunset" [@oweek-2014 p.102] [@oweek-2017 p.14], the 2019 glossary the **Acaterrace** with the same words, and the 2021 glossary simply "Fourth Floor Balcony" [@oweek-2019 p.14] [@oweek-2021 p.18]. The fourth floor's view was already a draw in 2007, when an O-Week Advisor was credited with "dropping 55 gallon water balloons off the 4th floor onto trampolines" [@oweek-2007 p.26]. The maps' "4: Patio", at the top of the laundry stack, may be part of the same balcony or a separate one [@oweek-2014 p.14].

## Variants & disputes

- **Backaterrace → Acaterrace (2003 → 2006).** The definition is word-for-word the same, so the space did not change; the name did [@oweek-2003 p.3] [@oweek-2006 p.83]. No source explains the change. One reading: Old Wiess had an Acabowl and a Backabowl, New Wiess one Acabowl, and the terrace was renamed to match. The maintainer's first notes, "aka the Backaterrace" for the servery roof, suggested that the 2003 name survived in speech alongside the printed one [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 terraces]; his second notes say that "Bacaterrace" for the servery roof "is just what Hanszen calls it" [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b terraces]. The likeliest reading is that the shared roof's Wiess name lapsed and Hanszen's stuck.
- **The books against present usage.** This is a real conflict, not a spelling. The O-Week books of 2006–2017 define the Acaterrace as the shared servery-roof terrace, "behind the Upper Commons that we share with Hanszen" (2014–17), and the Bacaterrace as the fourth-floor balcony [@oweek-2006 p.83] [@oweek-2014 p.102] [@oweek-2017 p.14]. In 2026 the Acaterrace is the small terrace between UpCo and the second-floor rooms, above the firepit, and the fourth-floor balcony is Toke [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b terraces]. For the present, the testimony of a current resident wins; for 2006–2017 the books are the contemporary witnesses and stand. Two readings join them. The books' phrase "behind the Upper Commons" fits the small terrace better than the roof, so the name may always have been used for both, with the books' writers (copying the 2003 definition forward) remembering only the roof. Or the name moved, after 2017, from the large shared terrace to the small Wiess-only one as "Bacaterrace" passed to the roof by way of Hanszen. Nothing in the record between 2017 and 2026 decides it. The earlier version of this page, which took the maintainer's first notes to mean that the servery roof is called the Backaterrace in 2026, is superseded by his second notes.
- **Bacaterrace: the fourth floor or the roof?** The fourth-floor balcony in the books (2014–17) [@oweek-2014 p.102]; the servery roof in Hanszen's usage (2026) [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b terraces]; still a current Wiess place name in the maintainer's first notes [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 terraces]. Hanszen's own website says nothing of terraces [@hanszen-traditions].
- **Is the "4: Patio" of the maps the Bacaterrace (Toke)?** Probably. The maps from 2006 to 2017 put a fourth-floor "Patio" at the top of the stack with the laundry (1), music room (2) and kitchen (3) [@oweek-2006 p.15] [@oweek-2017 p.28]. The only fourth-floor outdoor space in the glossaries is the Bacaterrace [@oweek-2014 p.102]. No source puts the two names together.
- **Backabowl or Bacabowl.** "Backabowl" in 1994 and in the 2006–2017 histories [@handbook-1994] [@oweek-2006 p.36]; "Bacabowl" on the 1999 photo pages [@wb 20020615221715 http://riceinfo.rice.edu:80/projects/colleges/wiess/college/photos/backabowl.html]; "Bacabowl" in the 2026 testimony. The same variation runs through Backaterrace/Bacaterrace.
- **"Unfortunately".** "that we unfortunately have to share with Hanszen" (2003–2011) became "that we share with Hanszen" in 2014 [@oweek-2011 p.92] [@oweek-2014 p.102]. See [Hanszen (the rivalry)](../traditions/hanszen-rivalry.md).
- **Which terrace hosted Jazz Night?** "On the terrace" (2003, 2006) [@wb 20040416083807 http://www.teamwiess.com/oweek/35-44%20wiess.pdf p.9] [@oweek-2006 p.44]. The large servery-roof terrace is the likely one, but the books do not say.
- **The owl.** Testimony only. Machado and Silvetti's and Kirksey's project pages, Rice Facilities' building history and the 1999 Rice News groundbreaking report say nothing about patterned brick [@machado-silvetti-wiess] [@kirksey-wiess] [@rice-facilities-first-100-years] [@rice-news-1999-10-14-groundbreaking]. Do not confuse it with the South Plant of 2009, a separate brick utility building "behind our very own Wiess" with "a tall glass structure" that "actually is an exhaust flue" [@oweek-2009 part 4 p.12]. Details on [Building lore](lore.md#the-owl-in-the-brick).

- **The swaps of 2019 and 2021.** Between 2017 and 2019 the two glossary names traded definitions: Acaterrace became the fourth-floor balcony and Bacaterrace the servery-roof terrace shared with Hanszen [@oweek-2017 p.14] [@oweek-2019 p.14]. In 2021 the Acaterrace moved again, to "the terrace connecting the second floor and the Upper Commons", and the balcony lost its Aca-name [@oweek-2021 p.18]. The 2021 entries are the present usage the maintainer describes, except that the book uses "Bacaterrace" for the servery roof without attributing it to Hanszen [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05b terraces]. So "Bacaterrace" for the servery roof is a Wiess usage in print in 2019 and 2021, not only Hanszen's.

## Open questions

- **The 2026 Bacabowl.** What and where is it? No written source after 1999 uses the word for a New Wiess space. Candidates are the lawn on the playing-field side, or the ground under the servery terrace, but this is guesswork; the maintainer or current students should say [@testimony-mullen-2026-10-05 terraces].
- **Toke.** Where the word comes from, and when it was first used. The 2021 glossary still calls the balcony "Fourth Floor Balcony" [@oweek-2021 p.18]; the 2025 book uses "Toke" [@oweek-2025 p.48]. The record does not explain it; the 2022 table is the earliest trace [@wb 20220709040142 http://teamwiess.com/images/oweek2022/wiessicles.jpg]. Alumni of 2014–2022 would know whether the fourth-floor balcony was still the "Bacaterrace" in their time.
- **The Acaterrace.** When the name came to mean the small terrace by UpCo—by summer 2021, per that year's glossary [@oweek-2021 p.18]; the 2019 glossary still used it for the fourth-floor balcony [@oweek-2019 p.14]—and when the firepit below it was built (it had representatives by 2025 [@oweek-2025 p.44]).
- Was the 2009 chessboard ever built [@wb 20090903015953 http://teamwiess.com/cabinetminutes.php]?
- When the Bacaterrace was first called that. The word first appears in the 2014 glossary, and the corpus has no O-Week book for 2012 or 2013.
- Photographs of the owl, and any Machado and Silvetti or Kirksey elevation drawing of the servery stack. Machado and Silvetti's page includes a full west-façade elevation (`1036-ELE-WestFacadeFull`) that might show it.
