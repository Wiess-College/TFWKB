# 2000s

The decade of the move. The website left riceinfo.rice.edu for teamwiess.com (announced May 2000, first content December 2001); the college left Old Wiess for New Wiess in 2002, with Colin Delany photographing the emptied building that August and a dedication planned for 7 September [@riceinfo-homepage-2000] [@teamwiess-dedication-2001] [@edesigns-old-wiess-2002]. The War Pig went from mylar heads (2000) to a Macy's-style balloon (2001) to a $4,500 commercial balloon that floated away at Beer Bike 2004, after which every O-Week glossary from 2006 to 2011 called it the "Former Wiess mascot" [@thresher-2001-04-06] [@thresher-2004-03-26] [@oweek-2006 p.84]. The Constitution was revised in February 2007, Wiess Day began in spring 2008, and the secretary's minutes went online as comedy [@constitution-2007] [@teamwiess-traditions-2014] [@teamwiess-lastnotes-2008]. The O-Week books of 2003 and 2006–09 are the glossary checkpoints; 2004 and 2005 were never posted [@oweek-2003] [@oweek-2006].

## Open questions

- When exactly did the college move into New Wiess, and did the dedication happen on 7 September 2002 as planned? The corpus has the plan [@teamwiess-dedication-2001] and the emptied building [@edesigns-old-wiess-2002], not the event. See [New Wiess](../places/new-wiess.md) and [Old Wiess](../places/old-wiess.md).
- When did Freshman Waiting end and service points begin? Between the 1998 website and the 2006 glossary [@riceinfo-cabinet-1998] [@oweek-2006 p.84]; see [Rules & Housing Rules](../governance/rules.md).
- Constitutional amendments between 1993 and 2007: the 2004–05 site had a Constitution page that was not captured [@teamwiess-site].
- The 2003 book's missing pages (15–34, 45–60, 75–79) and the uncaptured 2004 and 2005 books [@oweek-2003].
- Who were the Presidents and Chief Justices of 2000–2009? The teamwiess.com cabinet and court pages of 2006–2011 exist in the mirror and have not been read for this chronicle [@teamwiess-site].
