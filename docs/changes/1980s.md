# 1980s

This page lists what changed at Wiess in the 1980s, the decade Wiess went coed, met the War Pig, and picked up a pile of new parties.

!!! abstract "TL;DR: biggest changes of the decade"
    - **Spring 1983:** Wiess goes coed. That fall the women's teams are the "Battle Sows" [@thresher-2002-02-01] [@thresher-1983-09-30].
    - **1984–1987:** the War Pig becomes a thing you can see: a chicken-wire pig at NOD 1984, a trash-bag balloon at Beer Bike 1986, a 30-by-60-foot plastic pig in 1987 [@portal metapth245573 p.27] [@maxham-pig-document] [@campanile-1987 p.320].
    - **New in print:** Room Jack (1981), Big Bang, a party for new students (1984), and JamFest (1987) [@thresher-1981-04-09-room-jack] [@thresher-1984-10-05-big-bang] [@thresher-1987-03-20-jamfest].

Jeff Zweig '84 had been calling Wiessmen "War Pigs" since 1982 [@maxham-pig-document]. Sparky made the Thresher in 1981, and NOD opened to the Houston public [@thresher-1981-12-04-sparky-letter] [@ricenod-site-2005]. The print sources are great for the pig. For governance, we haven't found a governing document from the decade yet; the Woodson is the place to look [@woodson-ua0079].
