# 1980s

The 1980s made the Wiess that the 1990s wrote down. The college went coed in the spring of 1983; that fall its women's teams were the "Battle Sows" and its freshmen were taught to squeal; Jeff Zweig '84 had been calling Wiessmen "War Pigs" since 1982, and the name became an object — a pig-iron statue, a chicken-wire pig hung at Night of Decadence in October 1984, a trash-bag hot-air balloon at Beer Bike in April 1986, a 30-by-60-foot plastic pig in 1987 [@thresher-2002-02-01] [@maxham-pig-document] [@portal metapth245573 p.27] [@campanile-1987 p.320]. Sparky was built for NOD around 1980, and NOD itself opened to the Houston public [@handbook-1994 Sparky] [@ricenod-site-2005]. The print record is good for the pig and thin for everything else; no governing document from the decade survives online. The "Team Wiess" chant, which one source dates to 1984, is treated as disputed on the [1960s–1970s](1960s-1970s.md) page.

## Open questions

- The decision to go coed: who decided, and when the vote (if any) was taken. The Thresher of 1982–83 at the Portal would have it [@thresher-portal]; the 2002 feature gives only "spring 1983" [@thresher-2002-02-01].
- Constitutions and Cabinet rosters of the 1980s: none online; Woodson UA 0079 [@woodson-ua0079].
- The "Team Wiess" chant: the 1984 date comes from the 2002 Thresher and Zweig's fall-1983 freshmen; the 1974 and 1975 dates from the college's own later web pages [@thresher-2002-02-01] [@teamwiess-history-2005]. See [Team Wiess (the chant)](../traditions/team-wiess.md).
- When Freshman Jack, Pumpkin Caroling's route and College Night took their 1990s forms is not dated anywhere in the corpus.
