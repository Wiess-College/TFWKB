# 1990s

The 1990s are the first decade Wiess documented itself in forms we still have: the Rules of 1 October 1991, the Constitution and Bylaws of 5 August 1993, the 1994 Freshman Handbook whose glossary and history the first website carried, and that website itself, built by David M. Cunningham in August 1997 and rewritten by Ray Wagner in 1999 [@rules-1991] [@constitution-1993] [@handbook-1994] [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]. The War Pig flew for the first time on helium in April 1991 and was worn out by 1999 [@campanile-1991 p.281] [@riceinfo-beerbike]. The website's two rankings of traditions—Cunningham's in 1997 and Wagner's in 1999—are the best evidence anywhere of which traditions were alive, fading or gone at the end of Old Wiess [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@riceinfo-traditions-1999].

## Open questions

- Was the 1993 Constitution a rewrite of an amended earlier text, and what did the 1991 Rules replace? See [How the Constitution changed](../governance/constitution-history.md#variants-disputes).
- "Secession, 1992": documents in Bill Wilson's papers labelled Wiess–Hanszen 1992, posted by the Rice archivist in 2021 as "a complete mystery" [@rhc-2021-11-15-secession-1992]. A lead for [Hanszen (the rivalry)](../traditions/hanszen-rivalry.md), not yet explained.
- The 1993 attacks on the pig at Beer Bike and the letters that followed [@thresher-portal 1993-03-26 p.4]—a dispute between colleges, not a change at Wiess; recorded on [The War Pig](../traditions/warpig.md).
- Which traditions in the 1997 "Dying Out" list actually died, and when? The 2003 glossary is the next checkpoint; see the [Retired traditions](../traditions/retired/index.md).
- The 1997 site says "that supposed article of the Wiess Constitution" on Big Bang "is gone now? *sigh*" [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]—no constitution we hold mentions Big Bang until 2026 [@constitution-2026 Art. V §1 (2)]. Was there ever such an article?


[^magister]: Called "Master" until 2017; this site uses "Magister" for every period. Quotations keep their original wording.
