# 1990s

This page lists what changed at Wiess in the 1990s, the decade Wiess started writing itself down: the first rulebooks we hold, the first handbook, the first website.

!!! abstract "TL;DR: biggest changes of the decade"
    - **1991–1993:** the oldest Rules (1 October 1991) and Constitution and Bylaws (5 August 1993) we have [@rules-1991] [@constitution-1993].
    - **April 1991:** the War Pig flies on helium. "The War Pig flies!" [@campanile-1991 p.281]
    - **1997–1999:** the first Wiess website goes up, and NOD gets its first security rules and its decorations taken down [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@thresher-1999-04-23-presidents] [@thresher-1999-09-24-decorations].

The website's two lists of traditions, in 1997 and 1999, are the best snapshot we have of which traditions were going strong, fading or gone at the end of Old Wiess [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@riceinfo-traditions-1999]. By 1999 the pig was worn out [@riceinfo-beerbike].
