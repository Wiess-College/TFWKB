# 2020s

This page lists what has changed at Wiess in the 2020s so far: a rebuilt government, a cancelled NOD, and a Constitution that ate the Bylaws.

!!! abstract "TL;DR: biggest changes of the decade"
    - **2020–2026:** "Magister" throughout the February 2020 Constitution; by early 2025 a sixteen-article "Complete Guiding Documents" with the Bylaws inside and a Stance on Hate Speech, first announced in 2021 [@constitution-2020] [@constitution-hate-speech] [@thresher-2021-09-15].
    - **2023–2025:** NOD is shut down early in October 2023, cancelled for good on 5 June 2024, and replaced by a new public in January 2025 [@thresher-2023-11-01-shutdown] [@thresher-2024-06-05-nod-canceled] [@thresher-2025-01-mad-reviews].
    - **2024:** the inflatable pig is back at Beer Bike [@thresher-2024-04-10].

Most of this comes from the college's government web pages (teamwiess.com until 2023, wiess.rice.edu after), the O-Week books of 2021, 2024 and 2025, the Thresher, and the constitutions. The February 2026 amendment added only an Executive Cabinet and a rule for Election Representatives [@constitution-2026].
