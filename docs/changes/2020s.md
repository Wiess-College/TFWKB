# 2020s

The decade so far is known mostly from the government pages of the website — teamwiess.com until 2023, wiess.rice.edu after — and from one Thresher report and one PDF. The Cabinet of October 2020 already had Diversity Chairs, a Junior and a Senior Representative and an Apprentice Treasurer, so the Constitution had changed since 2017 in ways whose text is missing [@teamwiess-cabinet-2020] [@constitution-2017]. In September 2021 the college set out to add a clause banning hate speech after students were reported to Wiess Court [@thresher-2021-09-15]. The inflatable pig returned to Beer Bike in 2024 [@thresher-2024-04-10]. Night of Decadence was shut down early in October 2023, put on probation, and cancelled permanently on 5 June 2024; Wiess's new public followed in January 2025 [@thresher-2023-11-01-shutdown] [@thresher-2024-06-05-nod-canceled] [@thresher-2025-01-mad-reviews]. And on 23 February 2026 the "Complete Guiding Documents" replaced Masters with Magisters and folded the Bylaws' machinery into a sixteen-article Constitution with a Stance on Hate Speech as Article XV [@constitution-2026]. No O-Week book after 2017 is in the corpus, so the glossary series stops before this decade.

## Open questions

- **The missing texts.** Every constitution and bylaw between March 2017 and February 2026, including the 2021–22 version with the first hate-speech clause. The Google Drive links on the 2020 and 2023 Cabinet pages and the Google Doc on the 2023 page are the leads [@teamwiess-cabinet-2020] [@wiess-rice-edu-cabinet-2023]; so are the 2021–22 Parliamentarian and Secretary.
- **NOD 2026.** The decision record of that name describes the cancellation of 5 June 2024; the 5 June message itself, the probation terms and any Cabinet minutes on NOD from 2023–24 are still needed. See [NOD 2026](../decisions/nod-2026.md).
- **The 2024 pig.** Who built it, and is it the pig of 2025 and 2026? The pitch deck is testimony [@warpig-core-deck]; the Thresher is the only print [@thresher-2024-04-10]. See [The War Pig](../traditions/warpig.md).
- **"Core Team."** A page wiess.rice.edu/government/coreteam appears in captures from October 2024; its content did not survive capture [@wb 20241006035649 https://wiess.rice.edu/government/coreteam]. What is it?
- **O-Week books 2018–2026** are not in the corpus; the glossary series, and most of the "tradition changed" rows this chronicle depends on, cannot be continued without them.
- **The 2020–21 pandemic year**: Cabinet "now on Zoom!" is the only trace [@teamwiess-cabinet-2020]; what was suspended and what resumed is undocumented here.
