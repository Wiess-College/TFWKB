# 1960s–1970s

These are the decades in which most of what Wiess calls its traditions seem to have begun—the Acabowl got its name, *Hello, Hamlet!* was first staged, the WiessCrack was in print, Wiess students were pumpkin caroling, and the first Night of Decadence was held—and they are the decades for which the corpus has the least. Almost every row below rests on a later account: the 1994 Freshman Handbook's glossary, a 2016 Rice Magazine feature, the 2005 NOD website, an alumnus's shirt collection. Only two items are contemporary, a 1968 newsletter cover and a 1969 photograph, and both reached us through a blog. The Wiess College Records at Woodson (UA 0079) are where the Cabinet minutes and the constitutions of these years are; until someone reads them, this page is mostly questions [@woodson-ua0079].

## Open questions

- **Everything governmental.** No constitution, bylaw, rule or Cabinet roster from 1957 to 1991 is in the corpus. The 1993 Constitution's rule that a rewrite follows ten amendments implies an older, amended document [@constitution-1993 Art. XII §6]. Woodson UA 0079 [@woodson-ua0079].
- **Magisters and RAs.** Who were the Magisters[^magister] before John and Paula Hutchinson, "in their fifth year as Masters" in 1994? The 1994 Masters page does not look back [@riceinfo-masters]. See the [Core Team](../people/core-team.md), [Magisters](../people/masters-and-magisters.md) and [Resident Associates](../people/resident-associates.md).
- **The 1975 plaque.** Delany's 2002 photographs of the emptied Old Wiess are reported to include a dedication plaque dated 1975 carrying the Plutarch line about Socrates that also opens the handbook's page on Harry Wiess [@edesigns-old-wiess-2002] [@riceinfo-theman]. What was dedicated in 1975—the central-wing expansion Olga Keith Wiess funded?—is not established.
- **Dangling and Talleyboo.** Both were gone or dying by 1997 ("I'm glad 'dangling' doesn't happen anymore. Ask somebody who's already graduated") [@riceinfo-traditions-1997]; when they began is unknown. See [The Dangle](../traditions/retired/the-dangle.md) and [Talleyboo](../traditions/retired/talleyboo.md).
- **NOD's early years.** Kermit Lancaster's memorabilia pages are said to hold 1970s NOD flyers and shirts with themes and dates [@lancaster-wiess-memorabilia]; not yet read. See [Night of Decadence](../traditions/night-of-decadence.md).
- **The Thresher of the 1960s–70s** is digitised with OCR at the Portal to Texas History [@thresher-portal]; a search for "Wiess" by year would date most of the above.


[^magister]: Called "Master" until 2017; this site uses "Magister" for every period. Quotations keep their original wording.
