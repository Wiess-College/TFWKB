# 1960s–1970s

This page lists what changed at Wiess in the 1960s and 1970s, the decades when most Wiess traditions were born: Tabletop, *Hello, Hamlet!*, the WiessCrack, NOD, the Team Wiess chant, the College Idiot.

!!! abstract "TL;DR: biggest changes of the decades"
    - **1967:** *Hello, Hamlet!* opens, 5–7 October, written and directed by George Greanias [@thresher-1967-09-28-hello-hamlet] [@thresher-1967-10-05-hello-hamlet-review].
    - **1972:** the first Night of Decadence. The 1975 party was the "fourth annual" [@thresher-1975-11-03-nod-fourth-annual].
    - **1975:** Wiess wins Beer Bike (its second win) to "a chorus of 'Wiess Team. Wiess Team!'" [@thresher-1975-04-07-beer-bike] [@thresher-1985-04-12-beer-bike-history].

Until October 2026 this page leaned on later memories. Now most start dates come from the Thresher of the time, read issue by issue from 1956 to 1994 (see the [search log](../sources/search-log.md)). Governance is still to be filled in: the Cabinet minutes and constitutions of these years are at the Woodson Research Center [@woodson-ua0079].

The sources disagree on when the Team Wiess chant began: 1974 (the 2005 website), 1975 (the college website) or 1984 (a 2002 Thresher feature) [@teamwiess-history-2005] [@wiess-rice-edu /about/history] [@thresher-2002-02-01]. The Thresher has it at Beer Bike in April 1975 [@thresher-1975-04-07-beer-bike]. They also differ on who founded Tabletop: a 1979 Thresher history credits president Tom Bertrand (1967–68), but "Wiess Tabletop Theatre" is already in print in 1966 [@thresher-1979-wiess-first-fifteen-years] [@thresher-1966-10-20-tabletop].
