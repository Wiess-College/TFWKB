# 2010s

This page lists what changed at Wiess in the 2010s, the best-documented decade so far: we hold seven O-Week books and five sets of governing documents [@oweek-2010] [@oweek-2017] [@bylaws-2010] [@constitution-2017].

!!! abstract "TL;DR: biggest changes of the decade"
    - **2012:** the Class of 2012 builds the wooden War Pig. "The pig will roll" [@campanile-2012 p.172] [@oweek-2014 p.103].
    - **2016:** a new Constitution: membership set by the Dean of Undergraduates, search committees for the Magister[^magister] and RAs, Head Fellows written in [@constitution-2016].
    - **2017:** "Masters" become "Magisters" in the O-Week book, and Wiess turns 60 [@oweek-2017 p.20] [@wiess-60th-faq-2017].

Also: Wilson House was dedicated in 2011, "Club 13" became "Baker 13", Jamfest gave way to Wiess Day, the Hanszen insults left the glossary in 2014 and Dr. Bill's entry in 2015 [@wiess-60th-faq-2017 p.3] [@oweek-2010 p.92] [@oweek-2014 p.103] [@oweek-2014 p.105] [@oweek-2015]. The website moved to wiess.rice.edu in October 2014 [@wiess-rice-edu].
