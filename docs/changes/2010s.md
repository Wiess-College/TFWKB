# 2010s

The best-documented decade. Seven O-Week books (2010, 2011, 2014, 2015, 2016 with its Owlmanac, 2017) let the glossary be diffed almost year by year, and five governing texts—Bylaws and Housing Rules posted in 2010, College Rules of 2012, Housing Rules of February 2013, Constitutions of 2013, 2016 and 2017—survive as PDFs [@oweek-2010] [@oweek-2017] [@bylaws-2010] [@constitution-2017]. The War Pig came back as a wooden pig in 2012; Wilson House was dedicated in 2011; "Club 13" became "Baker 13"; Jamfest gave way to Wiess Day; the Hanszen insults left the glossary in 2014 and Dr. Bill's entry left it in 2015 [@campanile-2012 p.172] [@wiess-60th-faq-2017 p.3] [@oweek-2010 p.92] [@oweek-2014 p.103] [@oweek-2014 p.105] [@oweek-2015]. The website moved to wiess.rice.edu in October 2014, and the 60th anniversary was celebrated in November 2017 [@wiess-rice-edu] [@wiess-60th-faq-2017].

## Open questions

- Who built the wooden pig, and when was it first called the "Trojan Warpig"? The Campanile gives the Class of 2012 [@campanile-2012 p.172]; names on [The War Pig](../traditions/warpig.md).
- When did Jamfest last happen? The 2011 glossary still has it; the 2014 glossary does not [@oweek-2011] [@oweek-2014]; the 2012 and 2013 books were not captured. See [JamFest](../traditions/jamfest.md) and [Wiess Day](../traditions/wiess-day.md).
- Why did the Dr. Bill entry disappear in 2015? Wilson House had been dedicated in 2011 [@wiess-60th-faq-2017 p.3]; no source explains the glossary's silence. See [Wiess House / Wilson House](../places/wilson-house.md).
- The amendments between 2013 and 2016, and whatever followed March 2017: the 2020 Cabinet page's Drive links are the lead [@teamwiess-cabinet-2020].
- The 2016 Thresher report of a Beer Bike fine for the Warpig float ("its wheels don't turn") is cited in the War Pig notes without a URL; find the article at ricethresher.org [@thresher-web].


[^magister]: Called "Master" until 2017; this site uses "Magister" for every period. Quotations keep their original wording.
