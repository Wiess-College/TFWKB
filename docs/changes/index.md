# Changes

This is the chronicle: one dated line for every time something at Wiess changed. A building, a rule, an office, a tradition born or retired, a name, a website. Each line has its source.

!!! abstract "TL;DR"
    - Pick a decade below and scan the table. It runs from Wiess Hall (1949) to the Constitution of February 2026 [@riceinfo-history] [@constitution-2026].
    - The tradition and governance pages tell the stories. This section just gives the dates.
    - Before 1994, most dates now come from the Rice Thresher itself, read issue by issue from 1956 to 1994 [@thresher-1975-11-03-nod-fourth-annual] [@thresher-1966-10-20-tabletop].

## The decades

- [1940s–1950s](1940s-1950s.md): a dorm becomes a college.
- [1960s–1970s](1960s-1970s.md): most of the traditions start.
- [1980s](1980s.md): coed, the War Pig, and a lot of new parties.
- [1990s](1990s.md): the first rulebooks we hold, and the first website.
- [2000s](2000s.md): the move to New Wiess.
- [2010s](2010s.md): the wooden pig and a lot of paperwork.
- [2020s](2020s.md): Magisters, NOD cancelled, the mega-Constitution.

## How to read a table

Each row has a **Date** (exact if known, `c.` if not), the **Change** with its citation, a **Kind**, and an **Evidence** tag: [P] written at the time, [R] written later, [T] someone's memory (see [Evidence classes](../sources/evidence-classes.md)).

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| building | something built, moved into, emptied, dedicated or renamed as a place |
| governance | a Constitution, Bylaws, Rules or Housing Rules text adopted or amended |
| office | a position created, abolished, renamed or changed in how it is filled |
| tradition started | first appearance of a tradition in the record |
| tradition retired | a tradition reported as ended or dying |
| tradition changed | a tradition's date, form, venue or description changed |
| name | a name or term changed (the college, a mascot, a rival, a title) |
| web | the college's websites: launches, moves, closures |

When sources disagree on a date, the row says "disputed", lists every date, and links the page that weighs them.

## Greatest hits

Fifteen changes every Wiess student should know.

1. **1949–1950**: Wiess Hall built, "the first new dormitory in thirty-two years," and named for Harry Carothers Wiess [@riceinfo-history]. → [1940s–1950s](1940s-1950s.md)
2. **1957**: "Wiess Hall became Wiess College," with two resident associates and a student government [@riceinfo-history]. → [1940s–1950s](1940s-1950s.md)
3. **1967**: *Hello, Hamlet!* opens, 5–7 October [@thresher-1967-10-05-hello-hamlet-review]. → [1960s–1970s](1960s-1970s.md)
4. **1972**: the first Night of Decadence. The 1975 party was the "fourth annual" [@thresher-1975-11-03-nod-fourth-annual] [@rice-magazine-2016-wiess-traditions]. → [1960s–1970s](1960s-1970s.md)
5. **1975**: Wiess wins Beer Bike to "a chorus of 'Wiess Team. Wiess Team!'" [@thresher-1975-04-07-beer-bike]. → [1960s–1970s](1960s-1970s.md)
6. **Spring 1983**: Wiess goes coed; the "Battle Sows" are named that fall [@thresher-2002-02-01] [@thresher-1983-09-30]. → [1980s](1980s.md)
7. **1984–1986**: the War Pig takes physical form: a chicken-wire pig at NOD 1984, the first balloon at Beer Bike 1986 [@portal metapth245573 p.27] [@maxham-pig-document]. → [1980s](1980s.md)
8. **1991–1993**: the oldest Rules (1 October 1991) and Constitution and Bylaws (5 August 1993) we have [@rules-1991] [@constitution-1993]. → [1990s](1990s.md)
9. **1997–2001**: Wiess goes online: riceinfo.rice.edu in 1997, teamwiess.com announced May 2000, first content there December 2001 [@riceinfo-traditions-1997] [@riceinfo-homepage-2000] [@teamwiess-dedication-2001]. → [1990s](1990s.md), [2000s](2000s.md)
10. **2002**: Old Wiess emptied in August; New Wiess occupied, with a dedication planned for 7 September 2002 [@edesigns-old-wiess-2002] [@teamwiess-dedication-2001]. → [2000s](2000s.md)
11. **2004–2006**: the pig balloon floats away at Beer Bike 2004; by 2006 the glossary says "Former Wiess mascot" [@thresher-2004-03-26] [@oweek-2006 p.84]. → [2000s](2000s.md)
12. **2012**: the Class of 2012 builds the wooden War Pig; "the pig will roll" [@campanile-2012 p.172] [@oweek-2014 p.103]. → [2010s](2010s.md)
13. **2016**: a new Constitution: membership set by the Dean of Undergraduates, search committees for the Magister[^magister] and RAs, Head Fellows and Deputy Justices written in [@constitution-2016]. → [2010s](2010s.md)
14. **2021**: a clause banning hate speech added after students were reported to Wiess Court [@thresher-2021-09-15]. → [2020s](2020s.md)
15. **2020–2026**: "Masters" are "Magisters" by February 2020; the Constitution swallows the Bylaws by early 2025; the inflatable pig returns in 2024 [@constitution-2020] [@constitution-hate-speech] [@constitution-2026] [@thresher-2024-04-10]. → [2020s](2020s.md)
