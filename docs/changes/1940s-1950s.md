# 1940s–1950s

This page lists what changed at Wiess in the 1940s and 1950s, when it started as a dorm and became a college.

!!! abstract "TL;DR: biggest changes of the decade"
    - **1949–50:** Wiess Hall is built and named for Harry Carothers Wiess [@riceinfo-history].
    - **1957:** the college system arrives. "And so, Wiess Hall became Wiess College" [@riceinfo-history].
    - **1957–58:** the first College Night dinners, and Wiess's first Beer Bike title (in the beer-drinking contest; the bike race was rained out) [@thresher-1957-04-12-college-nights] [@thresher-1958-05-09-beer-drinking].

So far, most of what we have on the first decade comes from one later text, the 1994 Freshman Handbook's history [@riceinfo-history]. The Thresher adds a couple of rows from 1957–58. The rest (the committee's papers, the first Cabinet minutes) is at the Woodson Research Center, still to be read [@woodson-ua0079].
