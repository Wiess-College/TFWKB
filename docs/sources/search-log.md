### Before 1998

- The Wiess site's pages are dated 11 Aug 1997 and the Wayback Machine's first Rice crawl was 1997, so "before 1998" is a crawl artifact, not a start date. To find anything earlier: the Thresher 1994–97 for "home page", "Owlnet", "World Wide Web" (the Portal's bot-check needs a human click once per browser session); the `rice.*` Usenet groups in Google Groups (DejaNews coverage from 1995) for a "Wiess home page" announcement; ask the 1990s webmasters (Cunningham 1997, Abraham 1998, Wagner 1999, Leggett 2000); and the 1994–95 Freshman Handbooks themselves, which may print a URL or a gopher path.
- `riceinfo.rice.edu/maps/space/37/wie.html` (Facilities' Wiess building page, linked July 1996): check for 1997–98 captures — it may carry a photograph and floor data for Old Wiess.
- Hanszen's 1997 site (`www.hanszen.rice.edu/History/`, Cabinet minutes from 22 Jan 1997) is the nearest comparison for what a college web presence looked like in 1996–97 — and the earliest Hanszen account of the rivalry on the web.
- Gopherspace is unarchived: a pre-1995 "Wiess page" on RiceInfo would have been a gopher menu. Rice's own backups and the Woodson are the only possible sources.

### Method notes for agents

- From a cloud environment, web.archive.org, texashistory.unt.edu, scholarship.rice.edu and lancasterteam.com are blocked; run archive queries from a personal machine or a browser pane.
- The Portal's `/hits/` endpoint is the cheap way to scan a run of Thresher issues for a term list.
- `grep -i <term> sources/glossaries/*.tsv` answers "what did the college call X in year Y" instantly.
- Every corpus path is a citation: `tools/cite.py <path>`.
