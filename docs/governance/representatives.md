## An open-ended list

Since 2020 it's been "The Wiess Reps Program", which "gives the people of Wiess the opportunity to use their individual passions and talents to serve their fellow Wiesspeople" [@wb 20201009232352 [...]

So reps come and go. The 2017 list had a Cute Dog Rep, who planned to ask animal shelters "about the possibility of dog study breaks" [@wb 20170421224822 http://teamwiess.com/reps.html]. The 2025 [...]
