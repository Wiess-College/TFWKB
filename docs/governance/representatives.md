# Representatives

Besides its elected Cabinet, Wiess runs on a changing crowd of appointed **representatives** ("reps"): students who look after one thing, a room, a job or an event, for as long as someone wants to do it. The college website put it plainly in 2014: "Wiessmen chosen by leadership to represent Wiess in different ways. Need a board game? Contact the Board Game Rep. Have a food complaint? Contact the Food Rep" [@wb 20140627224604 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html]. Since 2020 it has been "The Wiess Reps Program", which "gives the people of Wiess the opportunity to use their individual passions and talents to serve their fellow Wiesspeople" [@wb 20201009232352 http://teamwiess.com/government/representatives.html]. Only a few reps are written into the Constitution, and the current text leaves the list open on purpose: "There shall be an undefined number of Wiess Representatives that shall include but are not limited to" Housing, Election, Merchandise, College Night, Webmaster, Chalkboard and Birthday [@constitution-hate-speech Art. VII §1] [@constitution-2026 Art. VII §1]. Positions come and go with the people who hold them. The 2025 O-Week book has two Fellows serving as "Wiess' firepit reps" [@oweek-2025 p.44] [P], a position on no earlier list.

The elected Representatives who sit on Cabinet (First-Year, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Off-Campus, and in earlier constitutions Cultural, At-Large and Capital Improvements) are on [Cabinet](cabinet.md).

## Variants & disputes

- **Who appoints them.** The 1998 Game Room Chairman was a presidential appointee [@riceinfo-cabinet-1998]; in 2014 reps were "chosen by leadership" [@wb 20140627224604 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html]; the 2025 and 2026 Constitution has each of its seven named reps "selected by the Internal Vice President", except Housing, who choose their own apprentice [@constitution-hate-speech Art. VII §1]. How the unnamed reps are chosen is not written down.
- **The Firepit Rep.** The 2025 book has two firepit reps at once [@oweek-2025 p.44]; no list of representatives after 2023 is in the corpus, so the firepit position's first year is not known. The firepit itself is in a 2025 banner photograph on the college website, captioned "Firepit Cab 2025" [@wb 20251013212528 https://wiess.rice.edu/] (see [The terraces](../places/terraces.md)).
- **Study breaks.** The Study Break Reps of 2005–06 and Study Break Coordinators of 2014 are gone from every later list; see [Study breaks](../traditions/study-breaks.md).
- **Yeah Wiess.** The rep appears as plain "YEAH WIESS" in 2010 and 2012 [@wb 20100826015923 http://teamwiess.com/reps.php] [@wb 20120817225627 http://teamwiess.com/people.php?who=reps]; the 2014 list glosses the "Yeah Wiess Representative" as the "T-Shirt Screening Representative" [@wb 20140627224604 http://teamwiess.com/representatives.html]; see [Beer Bike](../traditions/beer-bike.md#screen-printing-yeah-wiess).
