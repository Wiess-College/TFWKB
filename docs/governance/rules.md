# Rules & Housing Rules

Beneath the Constitution and Bylaws, Wiess has kept two short codes. The **College Rules** are what the Court enforces: a one-page list on property, noise, roofs, the Commons, guests and penalties, "Adopted 1 October, 1991" and "Last Amended August 28th, 2012" in the two versions we hold, almost identical in length (782 and 780 words) and in substance, the main losses being Freshman Waiting and the duty to sign a statement that one has read the rules [@rules-1991] [@college-rules-2012] [@gov-repo CHANGES.md]. The **Housing Rules** govern room draw—the "jack"—and survive as image-only PDFs of 2010 and 2011 whose text has not yet been read, and as a readable text "Last Amended February 6th, 2013" in eleven sections [@housing-rules-2010] [@housing-rules-2013]. The 2026 "Complete Guiding Documents" still refers to "the Rules" and gives three Housing Representatives the job of executing "all housing jacks", but publishes neither code [@constitution-2026 Art. VIII §7; Art. VII §1].

## College Rules, 1991 → 2012

The two texts share their skeleton: University or College Property; Search of Private Rooms; Excessive Noise or Disturbance; Unauthorized Areas; Dress and Conduct in the Commons; Conduct of Guests; Enforcement; Rules Orientation; Amending the Rules [@rules-1991] [@college-rules-2012]. The 2012 version breaks each section into lettered items and adds a preamble: members "are expected to conduct themselves, at all times, in a manner that will not discredit Wiess College or Rice University nor injure themselves or others… not only within the bounds of Wiess College, but also whenever on any University property or at any official University or College Functions off campus" [@college-rules-2012 Preamble].

What was dropped between 1991 and 2012:

- **Freshman Waiting.** "Freshmen shall serve at seated weekday dinners on a rotating basis… Any freshman not serving at his/her appointed meal and not providing a substitute shall be fined" [@rules-1991 §VI]. The section is gone in 2012. The 1998 website still had the Sophomore Reps "in charge of Freshman Waiting" [@riceinfo-cabinet-1998]; by 2006 the glossary explains "Freshmen Service Points" as "Four hours of required service for freshmen" [@oweek-2006 p.84], and the 2007 Constitution has the Sophomore Representatives organising service points rather than waiting [@constitution-2007 Art. III §4 I]. Waiting at family-style dinner ended somewhere between 1998 and 2006—plausibly with the move to New Wiess and its servery in 2002, but no source says so.
- **The tiled roof.** "Any person seen on the tiled roof will be fined, regardless of whether any damages to the tiles were made" [@rules-1991 §IV]. 2012 keeps only "Unauthorized access to any roof of Wiess College is forbidden" [@college-rules-2012 §IV].
- **Food fights and the Headwaiter.** "No food or ice will be thrown in the Commons. The College Court and the Headwaiter have the authority to advise and/or dismiss college members from the Commons" [@rules-1991 §V]. In 2012 the Headwaiter is gone, the Court alone may dismiss, and students must "clean his/her own area after eating and… respect both Wiess Commons & Housing & Dining staff" [@college-rules-2012 §V].
- **Flatware.** "Flatware and silverware shall not leave the commons area" becomes "shall be used in accordance with Housing and Dining Policy" [@rules-1991 §I] [@college-rules-2012 §I G].
- **The signed statement.** In 1991 every new member "shall receive an orientation from the Chief Justice and sign a statement that he/she has read, understands, and will uphold the rules"; in 2012 only the orientation remains [@rules-1991 §IX] [@college-rules-2012 §VIII].

What was added: the conduct preamble; a clause that anyone using "a Wiess facility (Private Dining Room, Movie Room, etc.) agrees to follow all rules of the facility, which will be clearly posted", and that "Non-Wiessmen who wish to use Wiess facilities must obtain the appropriate approval" [@college-rules-2012 §I H]; the Court "shall recognize University-wide quiet hours as outlined in The Student Handbook" [@college-rules-2012 §III]; the surcharge for unreported damage becomes "up to an extra 50%" rather than a flat 50% [@college-rules-2012 §I E]; and the Historian keeps copies of the Rules available "online" rather than "on a bulletin board" [@college-rules-2012 §IX]. The enforcement machinery—penalties set each spring by the incoming Chief Justice and approved by Cabinet, notice within seven class days, debts of $50 or less payable within two weeks—is unchanged except that the seven days now run from "the violation being reported" [@rules-1991 §VIII] [@college-rules-2012 §VII].

## Housing Rules, 2010 and 2011

Two PDFs titled "Wiess Housing Rules" were posted on teamwiess.com, captured 26 August 2010 and 29 July 2011 [@housing-rules-2010]. Both are scans without a text layer; their contents have not been read for this site and are not in the governance repository's text lineage [@gov-repo CHANGES.md]. They need OCR before anything can be said about how room draw worked at New Wiess in its first decade, or whether the 2013 text changed them.

## Housing Rules, 2013

The text "Last Amended February 6th, 2013" has eleven sections [@housing-rules-2013]:

1. **Housing pecking order**—seven tiers, from "Those guaranteed beds by the athletic department, University, or by the Wiess Constitution", then incoming students, rising seniors, rising juniors, fifth-years with under three years on campus, rising sophomores, and last fifth-years with three years or more; class standing by years at Rice, not credit hours [@housing-rules-2013 §I].
2. **The Freshman Jack**—two separate random drawings per gender, the upper one for freshmen "who have completed at least four freshman service hours and submitted their names to the sophomore representatives" [@housing-rules-2013 §II].
3. **Presidential Privilege**—"The president with a full suite trumps squatters and gets first choice of room at the college" [@housing-rules-2013 §III].
4. **Points**—rising seniors 4, juniors 3, sophomores 2, fifth-years 1; a group's total is the sum [@housing-rules-2013 §IV].
5. **Squatting**—a group needs at least 7 points among current occupants to keep its suite; "No one may squat on freshman suites" [@housing-rules-2013 §V].
6. **2-man, 3-man and 5-man suites**—a separate jack, highest points first [@housing-rules-2013 §VI].
7. **General Room Jack**—full suites only, forms from the College Coordinator, a $100 fine for withdrawing after the deadline, appealable to the Magisters [@housing-rules-2013 §VII].
8. **Leases**—signed online "on Esther" [@housing-rules-2013 §VIII].
9. **Appeals**—in writing to the Housing Committee "through the College Master" [@housing-rules-2013 §IX].
10. **Housing Committee**—"at least one Resident Associate and three additional students (one from each class)", appointed at the end of their freshman year for three years [@housing-rules-2013 §X].
11. **Parking Procedures**—the Wiess allotment of South Lot spaces, seniors first, with one space each reserved for the president, the housing coordinator, a social vice president and the college coordinator [@housing-rules-2013 §XI].

The vocabulary matches the glossaries: "Five-man—Rooms in the corner of Wiess that each hold five people" and "Room Draw—The process used to assign rooms for the next year" [@oweek-2014 pp.102–103].

## 2026

The 2026 Constitution keeps the Rules as a distinct thing it does not reproduce. Cabinet "shall set and administer the Rules and Bylaws of the College" [@constitution-2026 Art. IV §2]; the Court's Code of Conduct "shall be enforced by the Court. All members of Wiess College shall be expected to be familiar with and abide by the Rules"—a sentence carried over from the 1993 Bylaws' Article VI [@constitution-2026 Art. VIII §7] [@constitution-1993 Bylaws Art. VI]; and candidates "must have read the Constitution, Bylaws, and Rules" [@constitution-2026 Art. XI §3]. Housing is handled not by a committee of an RA and three students but by "Housing Representatives (3) who shall execute all housing jacks by the end of the Spring Semester" and "Choose an Apprentice Housing Representative before the housing jack who shall be a sophomore at the time of their appointment and will serve as Housing Representative and Senior Housing Representative the following years" [@constitution-2026 Art. VII §1]. The "Complete Guiding Documents" PDF contains no Rules and no Housing Rules; whether they exist as separate current documents "is not established" [@gov-repo v2026-02-23].

## Variants & disputes

- **When did Freshman Waiting end?** Required by the 1991 Rules [@rules-1991 §VI] and still a Sophomore Rep duty in 1998 [@riceinfo-cabinet-1998]; replaced by service points in the 2006 glossary and the 2007 Constitution [@oweek-2006 p.84] [@constitution-2007 Art. III §4 I]. The O-Week books of 2003 do not mention either; the end lies between 1998 and 2006, and the Rules were not amended to say so until 2012.
- **How many Housing Rules versions?** The 2010 and 2011 PDFs have different filenames and may or may not be the same text [@housing-rules-2010]; until they are read, the 2013 text is the only one that can be quoted.

## Open questions

- OCR the 2010 and 2011 Housing Rules and add them to the governance repository.
- Are there College Rules between 1991 and 2012? Twenty-one years with one amendment is unlikely; the 1997 webmaster thought a newer copy existed [@riceinfo-rules-index-1997].
- Did the Rules or Housing Rules change after 2013, and do they exist in 2026? Ask the Parliamentarian and the Housing Representatives.
- The 1993 Bylaws required a damage deposit from every on-campus member and a signed "Honor System" pledge about damages [@constitution-1993 Bylaws Art. VII]; both are gone from the 2016 Bylaws. When, and why?
