An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- A story is told that while Old Wiess was laid out, students moved the stakes for the foundation forms, so that the building was finished out of line with the campus ("crooked"). The teller has never seen evidence for it.

What would confirm it:

- A measured site plan of Old Wiess
- 1950s–60s aerial photographs showing its alignment with Hanszen and the gymnasium
- A written version of the story (Thresher, Campanile, RHC comment)
