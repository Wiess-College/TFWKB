An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- When a chair falls over in the servery or the Commons, everyone applauds.
- On Fridays music plays in the servery from lunch until dinner.
- Senior Debates are held in the Commons.

What would confirm it:

- A video or post showing the applause
- A Music rep or Friday-music-player description naming the servery and the hours
- Any written mention dating either custom
