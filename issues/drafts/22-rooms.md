An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- The office next to the Large classroom, formerly RUPD's, is now the Housing & Dining office.
- The 5-man suites are in the north-east corner: two doubles and a single, with corner windows and more space, since they need no room for a utility chase, neighbouring restroom or alcove.
- On the first floor the Large classroom takes the 5-man's place.
- Before 2023, by tradition, the fourth-floor 5-man was the President's suite.
- The first-floor "120" stack was designed as a resident-faculty apartment, so every room above it differs from the rest of Wiess.

What would confirm it:

- A current O-Week map or room-draw plan labelling the office and the suites
- Housing Rules or room-jacking documents naming the President's suite
- Facilities or Machado and Silvetti drawings of the "120" stack
