An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- DOME is a Wiess party held early in the fall semester, still running in 2023–26.
- The dress code: everyone wears black except the seniors, who wear red.
- A champagne custom is attached to the party.
- Gatsby is DOME's spring-semester counterpart.
- The 2008–09 website's pairing of DOME with Big Bang is regarded as unfortunate.
