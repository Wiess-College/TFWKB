An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- On Beer Bike, first-years shave their heads, or shave TFW, or a single T, F or W, into their hair.
- Others bleach their hair goldenrod, some only the tips.
- Someone usually gets the "grandpa cut": bald on top with a ring of hair round the sides.

What would confirm it:

- Dated photographs of Beer Bike mornings
- A Hair / Haircutting representative's description of the job
- O-Week book or Thresher text describing the custom
