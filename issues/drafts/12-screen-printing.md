An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- Screen printing T-shirts was once an O-Week activity and later became a study break.
- The old screens are still in the New Wiess basement, in the area of the former photographic darkroom.
- The best-known designs are "YEAH WIESS", "YEAH BEER" and "YEAH WATER", printed for Beer Bike.

What would confirm it:

- An O-Week schedule listing screen printing
- A study-break announcement for screen printing
- A photograph of a YEAH BEER or YEAH WATER shirt, or of the screens in the basement
- Cabinet or rep records of the screening equipment
