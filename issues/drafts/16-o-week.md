An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- Wiess's O-Week theme is "Wiess" every year, played on with a new pun.
- Puns remembered: "Take it Wiess and easy", "All you need is Wiess", "Wiess is good".
- The colors are always goldenrod and black: goldenrod shirts for new students, black for Fellows and Co-Fellows, and several bright colors for the Head Fellows so they can be found.
