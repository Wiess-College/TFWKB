An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- For years Big Bang served as a frank preparation of first-years for Night of Decadence.
- Students also call it "gnibgib".
- Since NOD ended in 2024 its form has been in flux: dance parties some years, scavenger hunts others.
- The 2008–09 website's pairing with DOME is regarded as unfortunate.
