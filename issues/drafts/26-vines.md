An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- Second- and third-floor residents complained about the shade the vines cast on their rooms.
- In 2020, with the building scarcely inhabited, rats took over the vines.
- The February 2021 Texas winter storm killed the vines.
- The bare metal grates that remain "enhance the prison vibe"; people now miss the vines.
- The architects' renderings show the screens green.

What would confirm it:

- Dated photographs of the façade from 2019–2022
- Facilities work orders for removal of the dead vines or for pest control
- Cabinet minutes or a Thresher article on the rats or the vines
- The Machado and Silvetti renderings
