An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- The Acaterrace is the small terrace between UpCo and the second-floor rooms, above the firepit.
- "Bacaterrace" for the South Servery roof is "just what Hanszen calls it"; an earlier note called the roof "aka the Backaterrace".
- Toke is the fourth-floor balcony, and can also mean the fourth-floor walkway ("Toke is canon").
- The Bacabowl is a current New Wiess place.

What would confirm it:

- GroupMe posts or flyers using each name with a location
- A Toke photograph that shows where it is
- Hanszen's own use of "Bacaterrace" in writing
- Where and what the present-day Bacabowl is
