An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- The custom of freshmen serving upperclassmen is remembered as "Formal Dinner", served at long tables.
- Wiess is "the only college that has square tables to this day", a survival of the seated dinners.
- The practice of sitting at a table corner is called "cornering".

What would confirm it:

- Photographs of the other colleges' commons furniture
- Facilities or H&D furniture records for the commons
- Photographs of family style in either building (Campanile, 1980s–2000s)
