An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- Die is a table game played at Wiess with one die and four cups of water, one on each corner of a table.
- A die is thrown into the air so that it bounces off the table.
- The game has "hundreds of silly rules".
- It is not unique to Wiess but is ever-present there.

- A written set of house rules
