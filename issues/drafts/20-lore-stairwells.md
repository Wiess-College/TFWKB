An oral account (shared with the maintainers, October 2026) says:

- New Wiess's public stairwells are known as A (north-west corner), B (south-west) and D (north-east).
- Freshmen are told that Stairwell C exists, hidden in the back of some rooms, closed off after "a terrible accident"; the story's origin is unknown.

What would confirm it:

- A photograph of a stairwell sign with its letter
- Facilities drawings or signage schedule
- Anyone who knows when the letters or the story began
